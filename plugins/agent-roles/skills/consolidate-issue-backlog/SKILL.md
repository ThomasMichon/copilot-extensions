# Consolidate Issue Backlog (Finite Role)

Make a selected backlog represent distinct unfinished outcomes, not simply fewer
open tokens. The coordinator owns cohort selection, evidence acceptance and one
publication stream; existing contributors retain implementation and resources.
Use [triage-issue-backlog](../triage-issue-backlog/SKILL.md) for individual issue
assessment or continuous intake, not bulk canonicalization toward a finite target.

Fill [the charter](assets/CHARTER.md) in the existing authorized effort/state home.
Read [cohorts and acquisition](references/cohorts-and-acquisition.md) before intake
and [acceptance and continuity](references/acceptance-and-continuity.md) before
dispositions or recovery. A charter records original authority; it grants none.
Reactivation needs a current charter, not another campaign's defaults or budget.

## 1. Establish one finite campaign

Resolve the provider, exact repository/backlog and supported filter through their
existing owners. Record the original request, baseline observation, explicit
target, inflow rule, excluded records, acceptance, publisher and closeout duties.
Do not silently expand a filter or convert a selected-cohort target into a
provider-wide claim. No numeric target is supplied by this skill.

Default to **read-only proposals** without disposition authority. Record permitted
effects individually: receiving-issue creation/amendment, comments, labels and
specific closure reasons. None implies another. Implementation, tests, rollout,
owner reassignment, claim release and infrastructure repair are separate scopes.
Reuse applicable original authority without per-pass permission questions;
ask only for a consequential missing decision or effect scope.

Read applicable harness/knowledge guidance, target contribution/review policy and
adopter eligibility rules. Record their sources, applicability, conflicts and
required unavailable bindings. An optional adapter's absence is a coverage gap;
an unavailable required reader blocks its dependent action, not unrelated work.
Do not install a companion or invent a transport, scheduler or permission broker.

## 2. Census and qualify before acquiring text

Enumerate the complete supported issue metadata with pagination, excluding PRs
where the provider mixes them. Capture query, observation time, stable identities,
original cohort, inflow, excluded and eligible records. Partial enumeration is
unknown, not zero. Keep the whole denominator visible when it governs the target;
ineligible issues do not disappear from that count.

Resolve actual authorship and source admission before opening report text.
Apply the target's policy to authors, editors, comments, history and related
sources. A metadata-only event actor is not automatically a text author; any
exception needs the adopted event-type/source rule, not a blanket bot exemption.
Roster membership is an eligibility input, not mutation or custody authority.

Recover previous acquisitions, refusals, claims and accepted evidence from the
canonical ledger before assigning work. Missing history or unknown consumption
is **unknown, not zero**. A new worker, context, URL or revision does not renew
spent limits or release a whole-source hold. Preserve actual restrictions without
inventing a universal collaborator-only policy or fresh per-pass read caps.

## 3. Select substantive cohorts and bounded research

Discover related work through available semantic/provider/local search, then
confirm current source truth. An index is neither exhaustive nor current-state
authority; unavailable search does not authorize service repair.
Group by common intended outcome, acceptance overlap and existing efforts, not
shared words alone. Prefer a strong existing receiver. Preserve oldest-first
coverage among comparably ready cohorts and record why a different cohort wins.

Distinguish duplicates/subsumption, partial overlap, actual prerequisites,
interfaces, incompatible alternatives and ownership constraints. Do not turn a
within-issue prerequisite into a whole-parent barrier. A themed graph edge is
discovery, not receiving acceptance or closure evidence.

Handle small continuous traces directly. Delegate only meaningful disjoint
research within the host's actual capacity and original limits; default to one
worker when delegating without a specified ceiling. Supply exact primary IDs,
permitted objects, existing holds/use, evidence gap, output destination and stop
condition. Workers return evidence only: no publication, ownership changes or
nested delegation. Use actual returned handles and native completion events,
not planned-launch rows or polling where notifications exist.

End each bounded study with supported completion, accepted canonical receiving,
evidenced obsolescence, or a specific **keep-open** gap and next discriminating
action. Do not force a closure for throughput or repeat no-delta studies.

## 4. Build and accept the complete receiving contract

Use the [canonical issue shape](references/acceptance-and-continuity.md#canonical-receiving-shape).
Map every unique source requirement and literal AND/OR choice to a direct
receiving clause or retained gap. Preserve incident evidence, existing receiving
text, unchecked acceptance, owners, choices and independently scoped platform,
native, operational and resource obligations.

The receiver must be actionable, not a title, graph link or "see source" escape.
A receiver can retain planned unfinished work; consolidation need not wait for
implementation. Conversely, completed source work does not prove a distinct
installed/live obligation or release a claim. Use the actual source request's
bar: do not invent native acceptance for source-only delivery.

Read the complete returned evidence and independently verify the route's
remaining acceptance criterion. Retain facts, hypotheses, pins, unavailable
checks and actual coverage separately. Worker completion, a passing test,
merged plan or receiving proposal is not whole fulfillment or accepted custody.
Refresh changed inputs and required claimant acceptance before publication.
Unchanged admitted evidence can be reused within its original applicability.

## 5. Publish through one owner and verify each effect

The coordinator is the sole publisher unless the charter names a separately
accepted publication owner. In read-only mode record proposals only.
For authorized live work, persist exact intent, refresh eligibility/ownership and
use supported provider preconditions. Never claim atomicity for read-then-write.
Land required reviewed receiving content and verify the actual accepted clauses
before separately adjudicating source retirement.

Choose the provider-supported disposition that matches the evidence:

| Route | Required meaning |
|-------|------------------|
| Completed | The whole requested outcome is fulfilled; acknowledge it and link delivering evidence |
| Redundant tracking | Every unresolved obligation is directly retained in an accepted receiver; explain that the work remains planned |
| Obsolete | Actual evidence or a scoped authoritative decision establishes why the request no longer applies |
| Keep open | A requirement, qualification, hold, claimant condition or receiving acceptance remains unresolved |

Closure reason names are provider-specific; do not mark planned consolidated
work technically completed. Another party's request retains its applicable
consent and disposition policy. Numerical pressure never substitutes for proof.

Read effects back under the original identity. Preserve unrelated assignments,
labels and discussions; do not edit another contributor's words. Separate
attempt, delivery, authored content, receiving acceptance, issue disposition and
resource release. Unknown mutation outcomes require reconciliation, not blind
replay. A verified remote effect with failed bookkeeping must not be repeated.
Keep public artifacts self-contained and free of private context.

## 6. Reconcile, journal and stop under the parent gate

After each coherent publication unit, reconcile a complete census and report
original-cohort outcomes, inflow and unrelated departures separately from
campaign credit. Journal substantive mappings, decisions, refusals, exact live
effects and retained gaps; avoid one documentary PR per status row.
Do not turn requirement preservation into an indefinite implementation program.

Use one existing observation owner and record its event/cadence/backoff and
actual watch/schedule handles if recurrence was assigned. Do not create a service
or per-worker pollers. A due wake permits reinspection, not renewed authority.
Slow review follows the target's normal watcher; it grants no reviewer diagnosis,
self-review or merge bypass.

Preserve the parent goal, original approvals/limits, current holds, ledger
location, acquisitions, pending effects, workers, claims and watches across
same-owner native continuity. Reload applicable guidance after reconstruction.
Actual new-owner transfer needs acceptance and owner-managed settlement;
delivery receipts alone do not transfer custody.

At the verified finite target, stop extra cohorts and side quests. Complete the
charter's remaining final journal, review and own-resource obligations rather
than declaring success from the count alone. If the target or another gate is
unmet, continue authorized useful work or preserve the exact blocker and return
action. Never discard work or another owner's custody to make closeout pass.
Installing this skill starts no campaign and changes no running system.
