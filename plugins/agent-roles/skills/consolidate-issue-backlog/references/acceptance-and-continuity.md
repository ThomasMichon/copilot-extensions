# Acceptance and continuity

Back to [Consolidate issue backlog](../SKILL.md).

## Canonical receiving shape

Prefer an existing actionable issue or effort over a new umbrella. A complete
receiver includes:

1. **Intent and gap:** expected outcome, current behavior and attributable
   evidence, with uncertainty clearly labeled.
2. **Scope and non-goals:** the union of retained requirements; preserve finite
   selectors, original alternatives and independently owned work.
3. **Plan:** bounded steps, actual prerequisite slices and responsible owners.
4. **Validation and acceptance:** every unique source condition mapped directly
   to an explicit clause, not hidden behind a source link or summary.
5. **Coordination:** existing claimants, decisions, holds, unchanged resource
   custody and required receiving acceptance.
6. **Traceability:** qualified source references, partial delivery credit,
   unresolved gaps and the reason each source stays open or may retire.

Use a requirement map with source identity/revision, literal requirement,
receiving clause, evidence/status, owner and remaining gate. Preserve meaningful
AND/OR choices; do not collapse "one selected target" into "all targets" or
"source OR native fix" into a single invented implementation. Include useful
incident signatures without repeating entire report transcripts.

Preserve the receiver's incumbent text, discussions, check states and owner
duties. Do not overwrite another author's contract with a generic template.
An aggregate can preserve unfinished work without pretending it is delivered.
Tracking consolidation does not appoint an implementer, resume a parked effort
or transfer execution/resources.

## Route-specific acceptance

| Proposed route | Verify independently before the effect |
|----------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Completed | Whole requested outcome against actual delivery; required review/checks and acknowledgment; only the request's actual source/release/install/live bar |
| Redundant tracking | Complete direct mapping, accepted current receiving content, actual claimant/consent conditions and current eligibility |
| Obsolete | Concrete changed condition or scoped authoritative decision; no unresolved distinct request silently discarded |
| Keep open | Specific gap/hold, current owner, useful next evidence or revisit trigger |

A merged PR establishes its own delivered scope, not an entire parent issue.
Source, synthetic tests, merged/released content, installed readiness and live
behavior remain distinct. Do not add a stronger gate than the original request,
or claim an unavailable tier from a weaker one. An old fulfilled request can be
completed only under its actual qualification and disposition authority;
do not use that as permission to duplicate-fold another person's unfinished idea.

Required plan review precedes dependent publication. After the receiver lands,
read back the complete relevant receiving contract and verify its current
acceptance. Then make the source-specific disposition judgment separately.
A receipt, reference, successful job or author's proposal is not receiving
acceptance. An explicit open-token or same-claim release condition remains
binding until its rightful owner resolves it.

## Effect journal

Use one canonical ledger, not competing live copies. Each effect retains:

- Original authority reference, permitted action and current limitations.
- Source/receiver identities and revisions, eligibility and requirement map.
- Exact intended mutation, original actor and supported provider precondition.
- Attempt/result identifiers, live readback and unresolved unknown effects.
- Distinct receiving, disposition and resource-custody outcomes.

Persist pending intent before effects. Reconcile a timeout through the existing
owner under its original identity; do not replay a possibly successful write.
Keep the provider's actual result even when local bookkeeping fails.
Local state or an archived artifact does not prove public publication.
Public/private traceability, AI attribution and sanitization follow the target's
policy; private context never travels upstream merely to make a citation.

Batch substantive accepted outcomes and journal them at meaningful boundaries.
Record retained open work as clearly as closures. Do not post repetitive status
comments or create serial documentation-only units without new useful evidence.
Repeated no-disposition studies require a changed discriminating approach,
another independent ready cohort, an event wait or a specific owner decision.
They do not justify weakening acceptance to improve throughput.

## Recovery, observation and finite closeout

Keep a concise checkpoint pointing to the canonical charter/ledger and original
authority evidence. Include parent acceptance, current cohort, spent/unknown
acquisitions, actual holds, pending effects, worker/watch/schedule handles,
claims, promised artifact disposition and exactly one next action.
Native same-owner context continuity preserves those live flows; it is not
permission to relaunch them or reset accounting. Reload applicable guidance.

On restart, reconcile surviving owners and effects before replacing anything.
Missing data is not empty-history startup; a stale duplicate ledger is not the
current source of truth. A new-owner handoff needs accepted custody through the
existing lifecycle, with original limits and obligations intact. Stop owned
schedules before an actual owner transfer and retain their rearm obligations;
do not stop another owner's work or clear claims based on process absence.
Delivery, consumption and acceptance remain distinct.

An authorized recurring census uses one existing observation mechanism, actual
cadence and bounded backoff. Prefer expected completion/provider events and
consume them without duplicate watches. An unavailable native wake mechanism
is a continuity limitation, not permission for an OS scheduler or helper daemon.
Routine slow PR review remains a normal watch, not a reviewer outage diagnosis.

A finite campaign ends only after a fresh complete census verifies the recorded
target and its other closeout gates are met. Stop extra intake/cohorts at that
checkpoint; inflow does not license inventing a new target or endless expansion.
Report original/inflow/excluded counts, attributable effects and retained work.
Complete the final reviewed journal and settle only genuinely resolved own
publication/resource obligations. Do not close an effort from a phase, PR or
count alone; unresolved work transfers only with the named tracking and actual
acceptance its existing owner requires. A blocker report or accepted handoff
preserves an unfinished campaign; it is not a completion claim.
