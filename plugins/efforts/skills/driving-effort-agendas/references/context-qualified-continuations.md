# Queue, continuations and context-qualified revisits

[Driving Effort Agendas](../SKILL.md) owns the optional action loop. This is
reasoning guidance, not an executable operation schema or another authority.

## Queue and saved continuations

Accepted ordinary work joins the tail, retaining order and canonical source links.
When the head item needs decomposition, create only its necessary in-scope
subqueue before the saved parent continuation. Nested planning preserves every
immediate parent, later sibling and original root; unwind one continuation at
a time. Do not recreate identities to discard prior attempts or shared history.

Conceptually: accepted work `A, B`; elaborate `A` into ordered work `A.1, A.2`
ahead of its continuation; after that work, resume `A` with relevant results,
then continue `B`. These labels describe positions, not new JSON fields or CLI
operations. Incidental discoveries remain parked until assigned or justified
as necessary prerequisites.

## Qualify a revisit, not a counter

Before revisiting the same stable item, identify:

- the actual prior attempt and what it established or could not establish;
- genuinely new relevant inputs/evidence, with their source and covered revision;
- the viable changed approach and why that information changes the next attempt;
- what remains unknown, including whether relevance is measured or agent judgment.

An identifier, timestamp, session, definition revision or freshness digest alone
does not supply useful context. Rewording the same evidence is not progress.
Equivalent numeric representations (`1` and `1.0`, or `0.0` and `-0.0`) are
the same input; booleans remain distinct from numbers. Omitting a known fact
and later restoring it is not newly learned context. Novelty must hold against
the item's retained lineage, not merely its immediately preceding fact bundle.
Declare raw source/configuration content as an `input` fact: its strings retain
significant whitespace recursively, while prose facts normalize cosmetic
formatting. Each subject keeps the policy established at its first lineage
occurrence; changing its kind or omitting it cannot change that treatment.
Exact input differences still require an attributable causal relevance judgment.
Structural acceptance can check supplied lineage/evidence relationships; it
cannot prove that a reasoning claim is meaningful or that an effect is approved.
Keep the judgment attributable and its uncertainty explicit.

Unchanged repetition first calls for bounded diagnosis or comparison at the
current position. Refine a viable approach, wait for a genuinely relevant
condition, or choose other already-authorized work while preserving the blocked
continuation. Do not repeatedly ask the Operator to restate the goal.
Involve the Operator only for an actual human decision, authority/permission
change or safety gate; honor explicit holds and unavailable authorized resources.
For preserved deferred work with an empty active stack, the declared source
`assign-root` operation can select a different already-authorized root with its
own baseline context. It cannot clone/reopen a saved item or reuse its semantic
alias. See the [worked deferred-root sequence](context-qualified-commands.md#work-while-preserving-a-deferred-root).

New context does not authorize a wider task, replay an uncertain external effect,
prove canonical acceptance or bypass resource caps. Retain original roots,
links, parent returns, pending decisions, shared visit/evidence history and
immutable accepted results across return, defer, restore and handoff.

## Execution policy and availability

Legacy cursor 1/2 and request 1 retain strict numerical admission. Their visit
counts include successful and failed entries; current legacy examples remain
finite count-limited fixtures. Do not ignore their rejection or loosen limits
through a prose claim that an attempt is new.

The declared source interface uses explicit `context-qualified` mode, cursor 4,
store generation 2 and request 2. Version 3 remains the legacy-migration refusal
marker, not executable state. Use the
[source CLI and worked continuations](context-qualified-commands.md) with fresh
owned state; installed copies may not yet support this contract. The source
examples discover live IDs and record accepted decisions separately from actual
attempts. The [managed command contract](managed-agenda.md) retains the separate
legacy and durability rules.

Resource/continuity caps remain finite and independently enforced in both modes.
Depth, deferred capacity, execution limits and safety are not an anti-spin score.
Successful child return alone does not reset `max_no_parent_progress` accounting:
only a qualified parent revisit with new relevant context resets that parent's
decomposition counter. Child completion is not causal-progress proof. Preserve
history and limits; further necessary decomposition may require bounded replanning
until the parent's new context qualifies.
Productive revisits under an available new policy need not stop at an old visit
threshold, but their cumulative history and all independent bounds remain intact.
Neither a count nor a digest is an Operator decision or an approval affidavit.

No global binstub, automatic turn driver, installed-payload update, active-lane
migration or polling service is introduced. Only explicitly owned adoption and
the existing review/completion/claim boundaries govern execution.
