# Prepare the decision before blocking for input

[Using Standing Authority](../SKILL.md) owns this procedure. A prepared packet
is a proposal, not [approval evidence](approval-evidence.md) or an effect grant.

## Continue meaningfully within authority

Freeze the actual unapproved effect. Before a blocking `ask_user`, use permitted
research, diagnosis, planning and documentation to prepare the operator's
decision. Preserve privacy/access and resource limits; "read-only" does not
authorize unrelated data access, disruptive scans or unlimited spend.

Autopilot is continuation, not presence or permission. Unknown mode/presence
does not justify idleness or a polling loop. Explicit all-work pauses,
maintenance standby and other applicable safety holds remain binding.

Keep the pending decision in the canonical effort/task or existing steering
surface. An adopted agenda may reversibly park its original branch, returns,
limits and packet reference, then select another already-authorized avenue.
Tree growth derives from the accepted goal, not unrelated work chosen to fill
the operator's absence. Revisit at a recorded condition-check point or an actual
new response; neither permits replay of a blocked or unknown effect.

## A self-contained return packet

| Component | What the returning operator needs |
|-----------|-----------------------------------|
| Situation | What is blocked, what still works and why it matters, in plain language |
| Cause and history | How the problem arose; confirmed causes separated from hypotheses |
| Fresh evidence | Current measurements, owners/consumers, coverage limits and uncertainty |
| Future projections | Measured growth/capacity implications and labeled scenarios/ranges; absent trend data stays unavailable |
| Options and ramifications | Immediate relief versus durable alternatives, costs, disruption, data/privacy risks and recovery |
| Recommended framework | How to solve this incident and its general class, not just the next tiny lever |
| Acceptance and next actions | Exact operations/resources/effects/owners/limits, required gates, repetition/recovery scope and what happens after approval |
| Preserved context | Original approval/exclusions, deferred return and what authorized work continued |

For a capacity problem, approved emergency levers may be exhausted while
content movement/deletion remains gated. Within permitted access, analyze
usage and legitimate consumers, measure net growth when evidence permits,
and propose retention/cache/ownership/capacity/monitoring changes. Do not
invent a forecast from one snapshot, mutate personal content or weaken a
quota merely to make the proposal executable.

## Ask only when the prepared decision is needed

Use a supported nonblocking pending-decision/steering capability when available;
do not invent one or claim the operator was asked when only a draft exists.
After useful safe work is exhausted, present the packet's recommendation and
scope choice, not a request to reauthorize the task itself or another incremental
lever. A real acceptance becomes the original bounded grant and is captured
immediately through the existing record/checkpoint procedure.

Avoid repeated unchanged questions. A saved "check again in four hours" is a
condition revisit, not a daemon, approval renewal or exact-turn guarantee.
Preserve unresolved responsibility through the existing agenda/handoff owner.
