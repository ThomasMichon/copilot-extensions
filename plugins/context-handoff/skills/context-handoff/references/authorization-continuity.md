# Authorization evidence through continuity

[Context Handoff](../SKILL.md) owns the carrier, not permission enforcement.

## Capture before continuity is needed

When the operator provides upfront approval or answers a one-time question,
stamp the original reference and necessary exact wording in the current owner's
existing checkpoint or canonical effort/charter. Record allowed operations,
destinations/resources/owners, effects and accepted blast radius, exclusions,
limits, retry/revision policy, validity and later holds/narrowing/revocation.
Keep agent interpretation separate. Do not introduce a parallel approval store
or copy private transcripts into public artifacts.

Use the host's offered artifact tools and actual revision/snapshot contract.
This plugin does not implement native checkpoint persistence or classify
operator messages as grants.

## Carry in both handoff shapes and native checkpoints

Every carrier includes **Authorization Evidence & Limits**:

- **Canonical reference/original evidence:** the approved effort/charter record
  or original operator answer, with evidence not yet durable elsewhere retained.
- **Applicable scope:** operations, destinations/resources/owners and effects,
  accepted blast radius, exclusions and current limits.
- **Validity and changes:** pins, single-attempt/use restrictions, expiry,
  current holds and later narrowing/revocation with their original references.
- **Unresolved:** genuinely missing decisions or unavailable evidence, not
  invented approval or an unqualified "the user said yes."

An effort-backed baton references its canonical record rather than copying the
whole effort. A standalone baton carries the relevant evidence and bounds
directly. Native rollover preserves them in the same owner's verified checkpoint
with the parent gate, live obligations and exactly one next action.

If the offered generator supports `authorization_evidence`, pass compact
references and exact applicable scope/limits after persisting the originals.
Its 8192-UTF-8-byte carrier may be reused by emergency drafts, but the cache is
not a durable grant or current-authority evaluator. Reconnect/refork loses the
in-module cache; recover the existing checkpoint/effort record and supply its
evidence again without asking for new permission. Empty input explicitly
clears that carrier. Older generators remain compatible: include the section
in the authored Markdown instead; existing storage preserves it.

## Recover without widening

A successor reads current canonical authority before effects, including later
holds or revocations. Use bounded source history only to repair a missing
original approval or later change. Cached text, a copied baton, plan, receipt
or tool success cannot create permission. A resumed source revision does not
itself consume an unpinned repeating scope; explicit pins, use limits and
unknown prior effects still require their original owner's rules.

Automatic drafts without evidence explicitly report **Not captured**, rather
than omitting the class or asserting no approvals exist. Recover evidence
before the affected deployment/content/external-owner effect; continue other
already-authorized work instead of asking for blanket reapproval.
