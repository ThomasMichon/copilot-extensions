# Second-Wave Scoped Dependency Register

Parent: [second-wave proposal](roadmap-wave-2.md).
Evidence: the [Bridge](roadmap-wave-2-bridge.md),
[Lifecycle](roadmap-wave-2-lifecycle.md),
[Transport](roadmap-wave-2-transport.md) and
[Worktrees](roadmap-wave-2-worktree.md) publication appendices.
Their underlying vision, effort and source evidence is pinned separately to
[`9dc3491fc527`](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/tree/9dc3491fc527bbf5561a082341c2b12c44343087).

These **32 directed proposals and two interface edges** are agent-recommended
relationships between finite slices, not issue-closure requirements,
whole-parent gates, accepted receiving transfers or implementation grants.
Every numeric endpoint links to its complete current issue in the appendices.
Named endpoints are the source's explicit policy, evidence or demand condition,
not new services or approvals.

Existing assignees and actual source/resource holders retain responsibility.
Delivered contracts can satisfy an entry condition without reimplementation.
Unknown effects, missing evidence, elapsed time and empty assignment never
authorize takeover, replay, cleanup or retirement.

## Cycles, incompatible choices and missing evidence

The listed finite directed proposals have no identified cycle. This is not a
proof that the whole backlog/fabric is acyclic or an endorsement of collapsing
each issue to one coarse graph node.

Potential #4876/#3905/#5472 bootstrap coupling must be resolved at the actual
already-owned toolkit/source-fallback boundary, not by requiring all three
parents to finish or creating a second resolver. #5543 has a genuine
BOTH/unused convergence policy conflict; #4173 still needs an authoritative
model choice before its actual migration.

#4040 exposure, #4102 scoped mux defaults, #3980 Vault target authority,
#3977 server-independent interpretation, #4053 lifetime semantics and #5833
raw-head scope retain explicit requirement-preserving owner decisions.
#4303's generic Worktrees AND CodeSpaces existing-branch route cannot be
transferred whole to a CodeSpace-only receiver. #4871 discussion is not removal
authority.

#4196 still lacks the supported external schema witness; #5330's cited active
home is absent at the pin; #3942/#3993 lack exact launch/parent evidence.
Current source-document historical wording about #5689 being open does not
override admitted issue history reporting it landed or release the independent
consumer claims. No linked-provider history or live operation was fetched to
manufacture those missing facts.
