## Requested outcome

Research the remaining issues against standing vision, identify high-priority
fixes, map them to efforts, and sequence the work needed for a stable harness
that progresses toward vision. Continue larger research waves without treating
issue-count reduction as proof of stability.

The parent's fixed checkpoint, author eligibility, full acceptance preservation
and existing resource/source custody still apply. Implementation and rollout
remain outside this research campaign.

## Research and coverage method

The mechanics in this section are **agent-recommended**, not additional
operator requirements.

1. Establish a fresh, complete open-issue metadata inventory. Count all issues,
   including ineligible authors and new inflow. Preserve the original cohort
   separately from current supply.
2. Reuse the admitted relationship graph and stable prior evidence. Give each
   issue one primary research owner; record changed originals, refinements,
   ownership and source state instead of repeating unchanged investigations.
3. Research disjoint waves, initially four sets of 50 issues. Combine oldest
   coverage with stability-oriented routing, but never use a title match or
   age as evidence of priority, completion or authority.
4. Before new content access, establish current author/editor eligibility and
   complete metadata provenance. Keep nonmember, unknown, locked or otherwise
   unqualified records metadata-only; record the reason instead of substituting
   another issue invisibly.
5. Compare the whole current issue and its refinements with exact vision
   features/behaviors, current reality evidence, and existing effort
   Plan/Validation/Coordination. Preserve literal alternatives, finite venues,
   thresholds, holds and implementation owners.
6. Integrate the per-issue evidence into a priority and dependency proposal.
   Review that proposal before any new execution charter, reassignment or
   acceptance transfer.

Every snapshot must account for the entire open set as researched, provisional,
pending research or excluded metadata-only. The first 200 assignments are a
research wave, not a claim that the remaining corpus has been ranked.

## Per-issue decision record

Each assessed issue needs the following information, with exact citations:

| Field | Required content |
|-------|------------------|
| Whole outcome | Original, refined and inherited acceptance, not one convenient fragment |
| Vision delta | Current vision path, stable item/heading and short supporting quote; closing, extending or below-altitude |
| Reality evidence | What is actually known, with source/release/installed/native tiers and observation limits |
| Owning effort | Existing canonical effort, actual status and phase, owner and retained obligations; a proposed gap only if no suitable home exists |
| Priority | Evidence-based impact, recurrence, blast radius, urgency and confidence |
| Dependencies | Actual prerequisites, coordination-only relationships, independent parallel work and unresolved cycles |
| First bounded slice | Smallest useful fix or qualification contribution, with its review and validation exit |
| Disposition | Still-open work, demonstrated completion, coherent consolidation candidate or unresolved question |

A missing implementation is not, by itself, evidence against thematic fit.
Conversely, an open umbrella, shared vocabulary or a merged planning PR does
not prove receipt of a source's whole requirements or completion of behavior.

## Priority rubric

This rubric is **agent-recommended**. It does not declare any particular issue
urgent before its evidence is assessed.

| Tier | Decision basis |
|------|----------------|
| P0 | Substantiated immediate risk to durable work or authority, or a confirmed failure preventing essential harness operation without a safe supported path |
| P1 | Material correctness, recovery, startup, delivery or release failures that recurrently block ordinary work or are actual prerequisites for other high-value fixes |
| P2 | Bounded reliability, performance, observability or usability work that improves normal operation without a demonstrated P0/P1 impact |
| P3 | Optional capability expansion, polish or below-altitude maintenance that can follow stabilization without creating a real dependency inversion |

Keep confidence and priority separate. A potentially severe but unverified
report may require urgent investigation, not an invented confirmed defect.
An old issue is not automatically important; a frequently mentioned subsystem
is not automatically the critical path.

## Sequencing rules

These are **agent-recommended integration rules**:

- Sequence against demonstrated prerequisites, not a blanket requirement to
  finish an entire parent programme before a finite child can proceed.
- Prefer extending the existing owner and building blocks over creating a
  duplicate framework or a new mega-effort for all harness work.
- Separate source correctness, qualified release, installed activation and
  actual native/adopter acceptance. A source fix can be complete while its
  explicitly owned adoption obligation remains open.
- Preserve current lifecycle policy, consent and recovery guarantees. Do not
  revive a superseded unconditional no-outage veto or weaken actual identity,
  health, durable-state and custody obligations to simplify ordering.
- Treat conflicting alternatives or dependency cycles as explicit design
  decisions; do not manufacture a linear order that conceals them.
- Keep independent contributions parallelizable once their own gates pass.
  A shared plugin name is not a repository-wide serialization requirement.

Candidate wave buckets may include authority/durable-work protection,
startup/recovery, release/update delivery, operational qualification, and
vision-expanding capability. These are organizing hypotheses, **not an
approved global execution order**. Actual edges and observed impact decide
which slices can start and which must wait.

## Integration deliverables and exit

- [ ] Full current issue coverage, with counted exclusions and refresh gaps.
- [ ] Evidence-cited high-priority shortlist; no title-only rankings.
- [ ] Canonical existing-effort map with retained owners and acceptance.
- [ ] Directed prerequisite graph with parallel work and unresolved cycles.
- [ ] Bounded execution waves, each with entry conditions, useful slices and
  source/release/native/adoption exit evidence appropriate to that scope.
- [ ] Normal reviewed publication of the resulting plan.

Completion of this roadmap does not complete the parent's numerical target,
any implementation effort, or a rollout. Those retain their own completion
gates and owners.
