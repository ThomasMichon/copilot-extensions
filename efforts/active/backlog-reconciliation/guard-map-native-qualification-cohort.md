## #10031 - Measure actual large-caller construction and cutover

The complete #10031 startup contract retains its whole #9911/#3303 lineage.

runtime/service lane for actual initial construction with representative
larger prepared-caller counts, normal and failed acquisitions, construction
and listener timings, serving continuity, bounded cleanup and platform limits.

source ordering is not that native startup/cutover measurement.

startup; it establishes a qualification question, not a deadline failure.

existing lifecycle owner. Preserve existing deadlines, acquisition diagnostics
and retry scope. Missing/unavailable maps stay fail-open at consumption;
uncertainty is not a complete negative inventory.

Git identity, caller/installation isolation, atomic publication, unavailable
versus complete-empty distinction, removal and bare/nonbare/nested/linked
layouts. Effective directional configuration, live modes, kill switches and
expiring grants stay authoritative. Preparation, managed mutation and explicit
refresh own acquisition; there is no event-time CLI/RPC/topology lookup,
watcher, TTL expiry or periodic topology recomputation.

#3303's independent full-harness objective still retains actual 20/20 clean
starts, complete default/all-plugin rosters, pinned CLI/payloads, real headed
turns, extension/hook retention, median/worst latency, phase costs and unchanged
deadlines. First-use readiness transfer, composed hook budgets, negative-work
controls, cleanup and one implementation per event remain separate gates.
