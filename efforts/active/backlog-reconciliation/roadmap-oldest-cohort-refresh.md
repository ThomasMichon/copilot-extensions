from the 2026-11-12 censuses, in five disjoint research assignments. Every original and
complete available discussion was qualified against the current complete
collaborator roster. The issue links below retain the literal requirements,
alternatives and original ownership; this summary does not replace them.

The research inspected immutable source at
[`a1907222c9`](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/tree/a1907222c9c88137d9b22009b18c1651816e83de)
and compared relevant files with observed Dev
[`8217680ea3`](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/tree/8217680ea347f478e2849cdada4cdb6b1ad606cd).
Changed source and later merges were inspected where material. Source findings
are not installed-runtime observations. No tests, native operations,
implementation or resource cleanup were executed by this research.

After the independently verified #1838 retirement, 71 remain OPEN. Many are
already the strongest cohort-sized canonical
issues, so moving them into another umbrella would fragment or hide their
remaining acceptance. A missing whole completion is not permission to discard
an idea, and an implementation park is not a research prohibition.
