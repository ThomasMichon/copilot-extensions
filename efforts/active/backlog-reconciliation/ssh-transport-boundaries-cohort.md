an OpenSSH baseline and machine-role adoption registry, Direct/DevTunnel/
interactive/dtssh/Cloudflare paths, tunnel-first firewall policy, local
private-key custody, public-pin distribution and durable trust fallback,
decay and healing. Coordination/identity protocols, a venue VPN and
per-host service ownership are not silently absorbed into it.

Credit the bounded #7206 outcome, partial #7222 work and #9051 audit without
calling the whole mesh complete. Six inherited families keep their own
scope, binding, custody and acceptance readbacks:

- #1647 requires actual Windows command, PowerShell and POSIX/provider
  OpenSSH behavior: shell-independent probing OR authoritative shell
  metadata, transport-versus-remote-shell failures, documented provisioning
  ownership OR explicit launchers, read-only nonzero unreachable results
  and regressions. Native acceptance remains distinct; no DefaultShell
  setting change is authorized by this study.
- #67 requires explicit `explore --adopt` OR accepted compatible findings,
  selected and validated through the canonical writer. Preserve identity,
  conflict and provenance, actual location/ownership, read-only ordinary
  JSON, unrelated profiles and pin consumers, and conflict/partial-result
  semantics. #7206's roster-default Bridge choice remains separate; no
  host-key, firewall or provider changes follow.
- #2203 concerns the exact originating Windows OUTBOUND connection to the
  peer, not a healthy inbound session. The expired-login explanation is
  unconfirmed. Keep the legitimate owner and only an approved present
  authentication ceremony/exchange OR an explicitly classified unresolved
  result; preserve the inbound session and prohibit disruptive repair from
  that same session.
- #1438 retains the SSH-syntax correction within the existing WSL restore
  flow. Timeout/cancellation must settle only owned child trees BEFORE
  retry/publication, clean both temporary families, preserve healthy
  fragments, and report read-only/nonzero failures truthfully. Deadline,
  progress, cleanup and retry need the actual Windows/WSL consumer, not an
  outer or synthetic proxy. This research runs neither a probe nor a
  cancellation.
- #4184 permits retirement only of authoritatively retired transport
  endpoints' owned pins. Preserve correlated, valid, unknown and current
  entries and strict checking; qualify the actual delegated algorithm,
  not merely its flags. This does not invent a fresh native portfolio.
- #5477 requires the default operation to refresh ALL aliases and obtain
  required live discovery BEFORE retirement. Unresolved discovery fails
  without route mutation; healthy no-op behavior stays intact. A doctor
  path is optional, not the default contract's replacement. Preserve the
  actual default consumer and successful refresh; historical native
  evidence is not a new reproduction program.

Existing SSH, provider, registry, key/trust, restore and transport owners
