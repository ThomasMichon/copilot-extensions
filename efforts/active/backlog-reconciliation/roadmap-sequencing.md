## A bounded contribution's exit

Select one residual with its existing owner, original acceptance and applicable
permission; consult the appropriate wave record and exact prerequisite row.
Then require evidence for that contribution, not a whole-harness readiness
claim:

| Kind of contribution | Required outcome before crediting that kind of progress |
|---|---|
| Investigation | Actual consumer evidence establishes a residual defect, credited existing behavior plus a finite qualification duty, or a named unresolved question. |
| Source repair | The focused regression and failure/refusal controls pass through the normal reviewed source flow; unrelated behavior and unsettled custody remain intact. |
| Release support | A containing normal release is identified separately from the source merge. |
| Installed/native acceptance | The authorized selected installation, actual serving identity and original consumer/platform/venue evidence satisfy that finite scope. |
| Tracking consolidation | Every unique unresolved obligation has direct receiving acceptance or remains an explicit gap; a source fix, thematic edge or planning PR alone is not closure evidence. |

The [parent Plan and Validation Plan](README.md#plan) remain open. Neither
completing a lane nor publishing this integration supplies implementation,
native execution, rollout, retirement or ownership-transfer authority. The
full roadmap, provisional decisions, continuing inflow and fixed whole-queue
checkpoint remain separate completion duties.
