## Cycles, alternatives and missing evidence

These scoped proposals contain no identified cycle. That is not whole-backlog acyclicity or an argument for treating each issue as one indivisible graph node. The Worktrees rows intentionally distinguish prerequisites and consumers inside the same issue. The #6383/#6443 query/backend and credential-delivery interfaces must not become reciprocal whole-issue closure barriers.

#7170 permits refusal before effects OR complete replacement teardown. #7805 requires fenced/returned start AND positively absent body before capacity discount. #7494 retains native ARM64 OR a supported trusted emulated base, while #7612 retains Windows AND Linux. Neither similar timing language nor a later unchanged pass resolves the distinct fixture, host, child and budget causes.

#6815 retains its explicit release AND deployment prerequisite; #7606 retains its exact affected-feed cutoff/consumer proof. #7237 settlement OR accepted transfer does not require both. #7944/#7945 retain their actual functional source/native admission, not an invented additional signature. #4564's original readiness/owner hold remains independently unsatisfied.

#5963 still needs contributed eligibility AND journal-production admission. #5993 needs scope/chronology reconciliation, not a restored managed-copy content gate. #6675 needs the precise original coverage witness or attributed disposition. #7238's native capability, assignment/feed and original integration are not created by a declared method. #6189's two designs remain undeveloped, #7949's actual staged source/result admission remains, and #7936's consumer integration is not established by its credited reducer. #6934 remains history-gated, unranked and without further semantic interpretation.

#6389's failed aggregate and disabled-task observation do not establish the inner cause or authorize task/service action. #8669's newly admitted refinement credits reported copy-only source completion, not installed/removal qualification; startup-root arguments and original literal survival outcomes remain. #7257's amd64 AND ARM64 qualifications are credited. #7091's latest native-history proposal claim was released without delivery. #8377's fake-ACP seed while queued is not native Copilot, START, verdict or offline isolation.

The parent Phase 2/2b/3, continuing inflow, provisional cases and fixed whole-queue checkpoint remain open. Optional efforts-owned agenda/helpers/nested return do not replace checklist completion or require Dispatch, a scheduler or a daemon.
