## #3940 - Preserve all nine owner-authorized cleanup clauses

Keep generalized runtime-artifact cleanup separate from version-directory
retention or blanket age-pruning. All nine refined clauses remain:

- Inventory declared producers/artifacts and owners: relay/inference inputs,
  process/routing/lock/registry records, logs, raw tails, durable user/session/
  task state and immutable slots. Explain payload-only non-applicability
  rather than inventing daemons.
- Define authorized shutdown cleanup, graceful drain, inherited/transferred
  custody, partial teardown and visible failure. Parent exit, absent PID,
  endpoint replacement or age alone is not disposal proof.
- Use the owning maintenance loop OR an explicit consumer schedule for bounded,
  idempotent, crash/restart-safe periodic cleanup; no parallel global sweeper,
  ownership store or service controller.
- Preserve token authorization, scope, lifetime and revocation without copying
  credentials into cleanup state. Expiry is not logical claim release;
  protect live, unknown, foreign and nonexpiring holdings.
- Preserve exact process/routing identity, namespaces, work history and
  publication custody. Duplicate labels, saturation and failed census do not
  authorize termination/deletion; sticky recovery evidence is not garbage.
- Retire logs/tails through producer/logger archive, every required publication
  target, retention and exited-writer gates, with unchanged revision/content
  and supported filesystem/native boundaries. Unsupported tails/errors remain
  visible.
- Keep immutable-slot current/previous-good, construction/retention, selected
  development and live/unknown-use protections separate. Receipt/configuration/
  session history retains its named owner.
- Reconcile lifecycle, retention, logger, shared singleton/replacement and
  conditional-integration authorities without taking claims or enabling policy.
- Preserve a per-owner shutdown/periodic/retention/test/adoption matrix for
  interruption, restart, new/concurrent owners, unknown observations, expired
  tokens with retained claims, publication outcomes and growing tails.

Credit bounded slot retention, partial conditional integration, sticky/
