This appendix contains **50 issue proposals: 39 researched proposals and 11 provisional records**. The [stabilization roadmap](stabilization-roadmap.md) owns cross-area ordering and integration; this appendix does not establish a separate execution programme.

Intent and effort references use the `307584006a70b5ca2de0ca82297fa71fc072f9b0` snapshot. Four narrowly selected consumer cases, including production and test text, were subsequently inspected at `d77783f0a10b067a249dd39cee09f48f9dc6024b`; their quoted files are unchanged between those pins. Standing visions and patterns did not change in that interval.

**Status is about research, not delivery.** A researched home proposal can still require an amendment or actual receiving acceptance. Provisional records retain real scope, policy, evidence or home gaps. Except for the four source audits below, implementation was not freshly audited; proposed fixes therefore begin with characterization or qualification. No tests, native operations or provider mutations were performed.

Each linked original issue is the authority for its **whole** contract, including refinements, literal OR/AND alternatives, thresholds, venues, claims and existing holds. The summaries do not replace those clauses or authorize implementation, closure, reassignment, resource release or rollout. A source author is not automatically an execution owner. There is no new blanket owner-ACK, store, authority or every-host qualification requirement.

Selected service replacement may use best-effort compatible handoff or authorized bounded cold recovery through its existing [lifecycle authority](../../../visions/plugin-services/README.md#zero-downtime-cutover). That is neither an unconditional no-outage veto nor permission to seize unrelated work, tenants, credentials or retained resources.
