## Standing-intent reconciliation

This plan follows the current installer
[authoritative replacement contract](../../../visions/installer/README.md#authoritative-runtime-replacement),
[prepared maintenance](../../../visions/installer/README.md#prepared-independent-maintenance)
and [single execution owner](../../../visions/installer/README.md#single-installation-execution-owner).
These are standing goals, not a claim that every existing implementation
already conforms.

The historical #7694 wording asking success to verify predecessor drain
**and exit** is retained as a distinct retirement observation. It is not
imported as an installation-success veto: current intent defines completion
by incoming readiness and complete current selection, independently of
outgoing drain, exit and cleanup. Automatic inhibition does not suppress
authorized explicit/manual replacement or ordinary healthy use. Actual
operator holds, incoming source/privilege requirements, real I/O failures
and resource-custody constraints still apply; this plan grants no execution.

Likewise, #7943's historical generation-suppression mechanism is superseded
by the reviewed direct-copy contract. Preserve its readiness/performance
intent without resurrecting skipped copies, preparation receipts or content
authentication. Every selected instruction file still reaches the required
copy boundaries; a delivery receipt is not model-loaded evidence.

- [ ] Keep runtime venv construction, deployment/start, redirects, cutover
  and draining out of the healthy foreground handoff. Nonblocking launch
  is not permission to disable updates, bypass admission/lifetime checks,
  spawn a competing updater or seize adjacent work.

### LR2 - One existing scoped runtime execution owner

- [ ] Reuse the canonical durable, on-demand coalescing owner rather than
  inventing a daemon, scheduler, updater or controller. Same-target requests
  share the existing operation and its observable result; distinct
  contexts/installations retain their real source, target, argv, options
  and authority rather than accidentally sharing a request.
- [ ] Coordinate with explicit updates through the existing owner. Preserve
  complete addressed scope/options and truthful pending/running/failure/
  terminal results; acceptance or queuing is not verified installation.
  Caller disappearance does not abandon the operation or permit replay of
  an unknown outcome.
- [ ] Preserve current runtime admission, governed dependency/wheel reuse,
  authenticated inputs and legitimate verified-source/local-venv fallback.
  No package, identity, retention or foreign-command policy is changed.
  Actual neighboring installer-context work and #6820 preparation/retention
  pause are not taken over or lifted.

### LR3 - Readiness, failure and independent retirement

- [ ] Prepare candidates without disrupting a valid healthy predecessor;
  activate only a ready incoming candidate. On failed background
  preparation, retain healthy ordinary use where available, settle the
  exact candidate through its owner and restore tentative pointers as
  appropriate. Preserve uncertain outcomes; never fabricate candidate
  termination or replay unknown effects.
- [ ] For an authorized explicit/manual replacement, incoming readiness
  **and all applicable current selections** define success. Best-effort
  incumbent recovery, legacy provenance/custody/capability, stale locks,
  queue fences or old leases cannot become a separate permission veto.
  Concrete conflicting writes use bounded internal join/wait/replacement;
  actual acquisition, privilege, I/O and incoming-health failures remain
  truthful failures.
- [ ] Retain outgoing drain **and exit** as separately reported retirement
  duties, not success prerequisites. Any independently authorized retirement
  remains installation-, role- and then-current-generation scoped.
  Session hosts, keepers, session-owned processes, foreground clients and
  protected connectivity are not fallback targets. Unprovable cleanup is
  reported and skipped, not turned into fictitious exit or update failure.
- [ ] Source design and contract tests still precede rollout under their
  actual owners. This plan is neither deployment authorization nor proof
  of source, release, installed or native acceptance.
