# Graph-first thematic consolidation

This is the execution slice of [Backlog Reconciliation](README.md), following
the operator's 2026-10-24 direction to widen research, sweep holistically and
build a graph around alignment and theme. The operational choices below are
agent-recommended; they do not change the campaign's scope or completion gate.

## Outcome

Make the backlog navigable as distinct unfinished outcomes and the relationships
between them, then retire redundant tracking through complete consolidation.
The graph is a research view, not another work queue, an implementation plan for
the products, or proof that related issues are duplicates.

## 1. Whole-backlog inventory

- Enumerate every open issue with pagination and a fresh collaborator roster.
  Admit each issue's creator/state/lock/assignees before reading its content.
  Excluded or unknown authors remain metadata-only, untouched and counted.
- Give every eligible issue one primary researcher from the same four retained
  researchers. Partition by broad outcome families, not consecutive blocks of
  twenty. Keep cross-family links available for coordinator synthesis; primary
  ownership prevents redundant issue research.
- Read eligible bodies and discussion to recover intended outcomes, refinements,
  prerequisites, explicit claims and holds. Reuse previous verified campaign
  research where current content is unchanged, retaining its observation time
  and evidence limits. New content or membership changes require admission.
- Preserve existing batches 91-92 as unfinished research inputs, without
  publication or completion credit. Import their findings into the wider graph
  rather than automatically beginning another narrow sequential pair.

## 2. Nodes and relationships

Keep structured graph data in the existing operational state, linked to the
canonical ledger. Use public repository identities for issues, efforts, visions
and supporting providers; do not create another scheduler or copy private
coordination state into public artifacts.

Each issue node records its observation, primary owner, concise intended outcome,
theme memberships, acceptance/custody constraints, candidate canonical objective
and research status. A theme is a grouping label, not automatically a new issue.
Existing issue/effort records retain authority.

| Relation | Meaning | Consolidation consequence |
|----------|---------|---------------------------|
| `aligned_with` | Shared theme or desired capability | Discovery only; inspect whether outcomes actually overlap |
| `duplicate_of` | Same unfinished outcome and acceptance | Candidate to retire redundant tracking after verified intake |
| `subsumed_by` | A larger canonical objective can retain the complete narrower scope | Requires explicit direct acceptance of every unique residual |
| `depends_on` | Completion or admission has a real prerequisite | Preserve separate dependency and owner; not a duplicate |
| `conflicts_with` | Alternatives or requirements cannot simply be combined | Retain the choice and route unresolved intent to its existing owner |
| `owned_separately` | A claim, hold or coordination token must remain independent | Retain the token unless its existing release condition is met |
| `supported_by` | Actual provider/source evidence supports a stated part | Record exact scope and evidence tier; not whole-objective completion |

Record the source and a short reason for every interpreted edge, with confidence
and uncertainty. Extracted mentions and semantic matches are candidates, not
verified relationships. An `aligned_with` connected component is not a
duplicate equivalence class; neither similarity nor transitivity grants closure.

## 3. Consolidate coherent groups

1. Survey all primary slices, then synthesize cross-theme groups centrally.
   Prefer one existing suitable canonical issue or Active effort. Do not build
   a giant miscellaneous umbrella just to reduce the issue count.
2. Distinguish duplicate reports, complementary acceptance slices, prerequisites,
   independent owner tokens and incompatible options. Preserve all applicable
   alternatives and unfinished outcomes.
3. For each candidate group, map every original/refined/inherited requirement
   directly to the proposed receiver. Supply complete Plan, Validation and
   continuing Coordination acceptance, preserving the receiver's incumbent
   requirements, completed rows, discussion and custody.
4. Recheck live author eligibility, source/receiver state, discussion and claims
   before effects. Deliver and read back complete receiving acceptance before
   superseding any source. An unavailable, incompatible or incomplete receiver
   leaves the source open.
5. Investigate actual full providers and causal consumers when claiming an
   outcome completed. Consolidation of unfinished work needs faithful receiving
   acceptance, not an unnecessary new product-completion investigation for every
   theme node. Never turn source presence into release or installed evidence.
6. Publish in independently audited units of 10-20 issues and run a complete
   queue census before the next unit. Group research can be much wider than a
   publication unit. Creation-oldest-first ordering applies within themes and
   among equally ready groups; maintain coverage for ungrouped or blocked nodes.

## 4. Avoid unchanged repeat sweeps

Use graph coverage and actual issue, dependency, provider or custody changes to
choose rechecks. Keep unchanged retained nodes visible with their precise gap;
do not automatically reread their entire implementation history each cycle.
New inflow enters the next inventory, and a periodic census still covers the
whole queue. Low expected closure yield is not authority to omit a requirement.

Report coverage, proposed groups and preserved independent nodes separately
from verified source closures and net queue reduction. Track which reductions
are consolidation rather than implemented outcomes; unrelated closures receive
no campaign credit.

## Validation

- [ ] Full eligible-node coverage is accounted for, with no duplicate primary
  assignments or silently dropped ungrouped/blocked nodes.
- [ ] Every interpreted edge has a cited reason; mention-only candidates,
  thematic alignment and actual duplicate/subsumption judgments remain distinct.
- [ ] Cross-theme links, dependencies, conflicting alternatives and live owner
  tokens survive coordinator synthesis.
- [ ] Each consolidation group has a suitable canonical receiver and complete
  original/refined/inherited acceptance mapping before any closure.
- [ ] Receiving readback, live author/custody checks, public explanations and
  between-unit whole-queue censuses satisfy the parent Validation Plan.
- [ ] The same four researchers and existing schedule/ledger remain
  reconstructible; no new fleet, runtime, product implementation or operational
  authority is introduced.
