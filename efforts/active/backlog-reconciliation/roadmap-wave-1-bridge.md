important qualification obligation rather than a new implementation.

the contract authority**. These summaries do not replace inherited acceptance,
alternatives, claims or coordination. A related effort is not accepted
whole-issue intake, and a file author is not an execution owner.

without changing priorities. The other 46 proposals are not fresh implementation
audits. Existing code, source publication, release, installation and original
native acceptance are different evidence tiers. No product test or native
reproduction was executed for this appendix.

## Bounded current-source audits

These are classifications of inspected boundaries, not whole-issue closure.
All links in this section identify the immutable source/test revision above.
Existing tests are **textual witnesses of coverage design**, not tests run here.

### Restart audit

For **5512**, the [actual restart branch][s-restart] calls PID-only termination,
then removes metadata even when termination returns false, and proceeds to spawn.
[Path-only cleanup][s-remove] does not compare the publication to the captured
owner. This supports a focused source fix: retain the incumbent on refusal or
unknown identity, and make cleanup conditional within the existing ownership
protocol. The existing [identity-bound terminator][s-terminate] is a building
block, not a missing capability to reimplement.

Credit the [exit-aware lock observer][s-running] and the successor's
[hard OS lease][s-lease]. A spawn attempt alone does not prove two active
residents; the old blanket existence-only diagnosis is inaccurate. The existing
[restart happy-path test][t-restart] is not the terminate-failure,
concurrent-new-publication or publication-failure packet. Keep the sole owner's
Phase 1 before Phase 2 and its original platform proof OR justified exemptions.

### Abort/arm audit

For **4619**, the [second validity read][s-recheck] is followed by session-state
writing, an [await before pickup][s-before-arm], then a
[separate final note/signal][s-arm]. The [file abort lock][s-file-abort] is not
held across that trigger path. The [task's expected-status abandon][s-task-abort]
guards its own store, not the later arm. The [actual ledger writer][s-note]
locks the Worktrees record; [opening/retrying a handoff][s-open] does not supply
an atomic backing-store admission for the separate file/task operation.

The [existing recheck test][t-recheck] deliberately narrows the earlier race;
it changes status before the second read. Propose deterministic aborts AFTER
the final successful read and before arm for BOTH stores, retaining candidate
custody, consume locks, task CAS and retry/manual-mode semantics. The exact
conditional task-arm design belongs to the existing store owners; this text
does not assert a new verb already exists or grant an expiry/steal mechanism.

### Consumer audit

For **5754**, [Bridge's actual prelude][s-bridge-prelude] reaches
[locked recovery][s-recovery] and its [guarded reaper wrapper][s-bridge-reap].
The accepted [Dispatch periodic loop][s-periodic] reaches its
[identity-qualified wrapper][s-dispatch-reap] and the
[same lock/reread/routing fence][s-shared-reap].
The [orchestrator][s-orchestrator] uses that transition authority for its run.
This is positive current-source evidence: **do not reimplement the delivered
shared-lock foundation**.

The [real helper-contention test][t-contention], [Bridge refusal test][t-refusal],
[Dispatch stranded-passive test][t-stranded] and separate
[Dispatch deploy-preamble test][t-preamble] are credited. They do not establish
the full requested actual Bridge CLI/prelude AND periodic Dispatch A/B witness.
Retain dead-owner recovery and non-deadlocking composition at their original
tier, with current best-effort cold policy. No further production defect was
established by this bounded audit: **qualification-only**, not a new lock fix.

### Framing audit

For **1803**, [TCP/Unix client constructors][s-connect] still omit an explicit
larger reader limit; [actual call reads][s-call] use `readline`. The
[warm CLI boundary][s-warm] catches `OSError`, not a large-line `ValueError`.
[Attach][s-attach] shares the same reader; [data listeners][s-listen] also omit
an explicit larger limit. This supports a bounded source fix over the existing
line-delimited protocol, not an invented protocol replacement.

Credit call cleanup in `finally` and the synchronous forwarder's
[repeated chunk pump][s-pump]: `recv(65536)` is not an aggregate frame cap.
The [small call][t-call], [attach][t-attach] and [forward][t-forward] tests do not
demonstrate a valid response above 64 KiB. Add that exact threshold witness,
consistent supported TCP/Unix/client/server behavior, honest oversize cleanup
and subsequent-call health. A configurable maximum remains preferred, not
mandatory. Never turn a post-admission framing error into a blind cold-path
tool replay.
