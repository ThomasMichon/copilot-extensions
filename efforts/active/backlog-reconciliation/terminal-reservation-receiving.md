## Complete direct AS-A9 sublane

### A9R1 - Capacity is not resource settlement

- [ ] Retain the completed/abandoned task scenario in which a reserving
  startup reservation incorrectly exhausts supervisor process capacity.
  Task terminality, age or observer absence does not prove an earlier launch
  can no longer finish.
- [ ] Reuse existing reservation, ground-owner, process-liveness and final-
  admission authorities. Separate process-capacity accounting from retained
  resource obligations; no reservation dropping, workspace recreation or
  new registry/lifecycle owner.
- [ ] Preserve task status/holds, workspace, native history, exclusive
  ownership and cleanup duties. Neither capacity discount nor tracking
  retirement releases them.

### A9R2 - Fenced startup and proved exact-body absence

- [ ] Discount capacity only with **definitively completed/fenced startup
  and proved exact-body absence**. Live, unknown, unreachable, missing-
  identity, legacy and in-progress starts remain conservative; insufficient
  historical evidence remains an explicit recovery blocker.
- [ ] Fence late-start admission/publication before allowing overlapping
  capacity reuse. Preserve ordinary queued-start conservatism and unrelated
  scopes; no healthy-worker termination or inferred absence.
- [ ] Retain the actual control path through `reconcile_reserving` and
  `fail_spawn(..., release_requested=True)`, not only a managed-start test or
  a successful unrelated task.
