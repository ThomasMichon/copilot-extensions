## Recover the existing rule, without opening held sources

The reviewed [semantic consolidation method](../../semantic-density-consolidation.md#cohort-parent-direction-2026-11-05)
already states that a citation alone is not an operator hold on an otherwise
eligible original. The role's
[acquisition guidance](../../../../../plugins/agent-roles/skills/consolidate-issue-backlog/references/cohorts-and-acquisition.md#acquisition-accounting)
separately requires preserving actual whole-source refusals.

These are compatible boundaries:

- Qualify the actual original, editors, complete discussion/history and any
  source being accessed under the existing policy.
- Preserve explicit direct operator/owner restrictions, actual provenance
  refusals and content exclusions. Do not fetch or reconstruct a held target
  through another copy, excerpt, diff, quotation or index.
- A reference to a held target supplies neither target access nor its missing
  acceptance clauses. Record an unresolved dependency where that evidence is
  needed; do not turn lexical citation propagation into a new operator ban
  on independently eligible outer text.
- Record a parent decision for each requested unit before rendering. Missing,
  unresolved, wrong-identity, incomplete or stale admission refuses without
  falling back to an older helper or emitting a title first.
- Keep independent evidence tiers: acquired bytes, model-read complete units,
  whole-contract mappings, accepted receiving and technical fulfillment.

This applies existing intent; it grants no new access, releases no underlying
source hold and creates no retrospective compliance. Governing guidance
remains distinct from product/receiving evidence.

The coordinator qualified a local research-only reader with inert fixtures.
Missing/held/unresolved decisions refuse before packet access; stale decisions
refuse before original access; positive fixtures retain the entire original,
edits, comments and comment edits; bounded chunks reassemble losslessly.
This is not shipped product code or a product/native test result.
