## #14291: one owner per fleet effect

The [whole role-ownership original](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/issues/14291)
and [maintenance-window refinement](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/issues/14291#issuecomment-7116227334)
retain a complete three-role separation:

- Dispatcher translates aggregate operator goals into work, worktree and
  machine placement; creates worktrees, charters and standing charters; sets
  priority, performs the first launch and assigns/transfers duties. It does
  not stop, restart or recover worktrees.
- Monitor keeps assigned agents alive/on charter, steers and owns same-session
  R3 recovery. Seeded same-worktree R4 needs its granted class; no second live
  head, new recovery worktree or discarded work. The monitor executes planned
  persistent-agent lifecycle, checks the old owner's disposition after a duty
  transfer and owns the actual-state fleet register.
- Maintainer owns one host's runtime/services, system updates, crash analysis,
  unblocking repair and host-ready declaration, not starting/stopping agents.
  The dispatcher owns intended assignments; the monitor joins that map to its
  actual register each cycle and reports mismatches and starved work.

Preserve assign/run/transfer/close, with old work done, reassigned or finalized
by its owner rather than discarded. Confirm absence with native readers:
errors/timeouts do not prove absence. After a crash, await the maintainer's
host-ready declaration; the original allows proceeding on a responsive host
without a maintainer. On saturated hosts recover one agent at a time and verify
live/seeded state before reporting recovery.

The refinement's disruptive-maintenance sequence remains request(host, reason,
earliest time, expected duration), monitor safe-checkpoint quiescence and
"stopped for maintenance" records, maintainer update/verification/host-ready,
then monitor restoration and verification of every agent it stopped.
Nondisruptive updates need no monitor step.

Acceptance covers the whole matrix across README, role skills/charters and
named references, plus standing-charter documentation; dormant correction and
R4 must agree, no dispatcher stop/restart/recover instruction or maintainer
agent-start/stop instruction remains, and agent-roles/agent-dispatch tests pass.

this lands, not new implementation or an automatically filed issue here.

## #14303 and #14304: prerequisite versus broader lifecycle

Its description and implementation were not read; an open source PR does not

[#14304](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/issues/14304)
and its [Bridge constraint](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/issues/14304#issuecomment-7118554905)
retain a broader seven-stage lifecycle:

1. Pool assignment immediately creates a reservation and allocated worktree id,
   with no session yet, line-entry time and task `claimed`.
2. Capacity grants slots to oldest waiting reservations until full.
3. Granted work starts/resumes through Bridge according to retained session/
   worktree state; boot/task acquisition records task `started`.
4. `suspended` or `submitted` enters pending shutdown.
5. After configurable grace, default five minutes, Bridge stops the agent.
6. Only confirmed stop releases the slot, preserving worktree and session.
7. Wake from either state returns to `claimed` with fresh line-entry time;
   fresh placement and wakes share one ordered reservation line.

The original explicitly separates the owner-gone repair, grace-period cooling
of suspended/submitted bodies including CLI-native sessions, and ordered-line
unification. The refinement requires every CLI worker launch/resume/stop through
Bridge CLI-mode create/acquire/resume/stop, not Worktrees `embody`. Preserve the
receipt-recorded Bridge session identity rather than confusing it with the
native conversation id. Its migration inventory includes embodied/fleet worker
spawners, satellite/interactive/fleet entry, create/remote-create and recipe kick,
stalled-handoff resume attention and standing-recovery dry-run preflight.

Therefore #14303 is a bounded prerequisite, not a duplicate of the whole
#14304 outcome. The latter's short reference does not directly receive every
transaction/negative guard from the former. No complete accepted receiving
or owner transfer is established, so neither token is folded by theme.

### Later actual-state reconciliation refinement

The [new whole discussion](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/issues/14304#issuecomment-7120227886)
was acquired after complete current authored metadata, the unchanged full
roster and all five timeline events qualified, with stable guards around
acquisition. The original and first discussion were reused, not reread.
It adds rules for reservation records that disagree with actual agent state:

- A cold reservation whose agent is alive is not cold: do not force-start it
  or retroactively charge it to a pool slot.
- A live reservation that holds a slot but whose agent is dead is restarted
  by its supervisor. Exhausting the attempt limit frees the slot and begins
  the attempt-limit cooldown.
- A suspended task gets a short grace period, about five minutes, before
  Bridge stops its body. Confirmed shutdown precedes slot release and starting
  the next ready-as-of reservation.

The refinement names three mechanisms, with the same rules in each: periodic
supervisor queries of the assigned pool's actual Bridge/Worktrees state;
Dispatch registration of an activation-event hook with Worktrees; and an
actual-state read/record at each reservation operation. A start requested for
an already-running agent becomes a steer/nudge, while an already-down stop
reconciles the final states.

Retain Bridge CLI-mode create/resume rather than Worktrees `embody`, allocation
of a worktree-bound reservation at pool assignment, and ready-as-of ordering.
The author separately reports a cooler limited to pool-bound
`fleet-body:`/`local-body:` handles without grace, and an unbound reservation
left `spawned` after suspension with no reconciliation. Those are attributed
source observations, not campaign reproduction or proof of the complete
implementation gap.

This design refinement is not an implicit rewrite of the separately mapped
#14303 transaction and exclusions. Its live-slot restart rule, cold-but-alive
rule and the earlier owner-gone/cooling boundaries need consistent acceptance
in the existing implementation lane; shared vocabulary does not prove that
integration or transfer every clause into #14304. Neither an unconfirmed probe
nor this research authorizes a restart, stop, slot release or new recovery actor.
