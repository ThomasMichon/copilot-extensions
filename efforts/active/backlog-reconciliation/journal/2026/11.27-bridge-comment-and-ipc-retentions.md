## Bridge: disabled task is not runtime-only construction

The whole #9357 original and its discussion retain these separate requirements:

- Distinguish built/selected runtime state, service activation and maintenance
  inhibition; explicitly handle enabled, disabled and absent task registrations.
- Preserve independently explicit OS/service disablement. Neither silently
  re-enabling a task nor direct-start fallback may bypass that intent.
- Keep cooperative maintenance runnable but quiescent, then resume its existing
  loops/subscriptions after applicable clearance without duplicate workers.
- Keep uninstall deregistration, normal update registration preservation and
  maintenance distinct; leave critical connectivity and independently hosted
  sessions untouched.
- Preserve the original diagnostic uncertainty. The first selected-runtime
  observation differs from the later completed-but-unselected candidate.
  #8370/#8826 own missing inner exceptions; #9567 separately tracks Task-broker
  adoption calls.

#12770 instead requests explicit closed-wheel runtime-only construction/reuse,
validation, selection and publication without service reconciliation,
start/stop or scheduled-task hold changes. The caller retains restoration of
only previously running services and final serving verification. Ordinary
standalone behavior must remain unchanged, and unsupported/conflicting requests
must refuse before effects.

Qualified complete #12782 describes `install`/`update -NoService` at the
existing Bridge provider and synchronized native driver. It actually merged
to Dev as `2ed44765d4b0a4d6590f629620926755b6b5ac19`.
Its description distinguishes runtime readiness from caller adoption,
closed-wheel first touch, service restoration and installed-release

This study did not establish the complete native production/test delta or

## IPC: larger server limits do not fix default-limit clients

Whole #1803 and both discussions preserve the original minimum alternative:
consistent appropriate TCP/Unix reader limits and a valid response above
64 KiB regression. A configurable maximum is preferred, not a newly mandatory
protocol redesign. Bounded oversize/cleanup behavior, coherent call/forward/
attach handling and subsequent-call health without ambiguous tool replay
remain acceptance conditions.

The current IPC blob differs from the older inspected one. Exact native
producer PRs #11911/#11935 qualify before the changed paths are read.
At the same inspected source:

- [`_connect`](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/blob/5a3e2bd5da169bab9bd3a50a2d2bcf270a7ca770/plugins/agent-mcp/src/agent_mcp/ipc.py#L90-L123)
  accepts an optional `limit`, but defaults it to `None`, which forwards no
  limit option to either asyncio TCP or Unix connection constructor.
- [`request_via_socket`](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/blob/5a3e2bd5da169bab9bd3a50a2d2bcf270a7ca770/plugins/agent-mcp/src/agent_mcp/ipc.py#L125-L166)
  and [async attach](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/blob/5a3e2bd5da169bab9bd3a50a2d2bcf270a7ca770/plugins/agent-mcp/src/agent_mcp/ipc.py#L232-L272)
  still call `_connect(socket_path)` without supplying that option, then read
  a line. The default client-reader limit remains a concrete counterexample.
- [Data listeners](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/blob/5a3e2bd5da169bab9bd3a50a2d2bcf270a7ca770/plugins/agent-mcp/src/agent_mcp/serve.py#L640-L664)
  now pass `MAX_FRAME`. This positive server-side change is not client-side
  admission.
- A sent warm call's read failure becomes `OutcomeUnknown` without cold replay,
  and the writer is closed. That positive change still does not deliver a valid
  large result.
- The synchronous attach acknowledgment has a separate byte budget, while
  [`pump`](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/blob/5a3e2bd5da169bab9bd3a50a2d2bcf270a7ca770/plugins/agent-mcp/src/agent_mcp/sockio.py#L301-L346)
  repeatedly receives 65536-byte chunks until EOF. That chunk size is not a
  fixed maximum tool-response size.

No product import, warm-host call, listener, native regression or service
operation was performed. The source counterexample keeps this useful bug OPEN;
no unrelated initialization symptom or tool-output failure is assigned to it.
