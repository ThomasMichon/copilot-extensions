## Why this is a receiver, not another study

The canonical body carries the finite planned work rather than linking five
unresolved reports from a generic umbrella. It credits the existing partial
deliveries and explicitly retains privacy, consent, negative-access,
real-caller and assertion-preservation requirements.

The work slices remain independently implementable. Only actual local
dependencies are asserted: safe diagnostic construction before publication,
descriptor/access qualification before enabling Windows export, and
cause-preserving resolution before certifying handoff success. There is no
invented common blocker, implementation appointment, operational permission
or universal native qualification gate.

The receiver's live body was compared with the complete publication draft.
All 21 rows remained unchecked after all five leaf closures. Each public
acknowledgment was read back exactly, and complete post-effect metadata
confirmed the closed state, eligible provenance and unchanged source
assignees. Close mutations requested metadata only, not another copy of an
original body.
