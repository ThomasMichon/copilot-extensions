their distinct original scopes. No referenced body or protected source is
admitted merely through an issue link. Unknown or unreadable process/content

or absence of a visible process is not disposal authority.

claims, holds, deadlines and approval limits remain intact. This campaign ran
no product tests, native process, cleanup, installer or deployment. Actual
implementation and any authorized physical reclamation remain separate work.
