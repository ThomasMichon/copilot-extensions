## Nonedges and unresolved decisions

These are scoped facts and proposed interfaces, not an unconditional issue-level
DAG. Same-issue containment/policy edges above are slice dependencies, not cycles
requiring whole-issue completion. No whole-issue cycle is asserted by this
first-wave register; later-wave or interface conflicts remain possible.

The #3698 lease alternative, #4130 terminal-fact policy and #5839
success-versus-startup linkage remain explicit decisions with their current
owners. A roadmap does not resolve them by choosing whichever alternative is
easier to implement. Likewise, #3444 and #5356 historical uncertainty cannot
be collapsed into one assumed stale-cache cause.

Independent source fixes, contained regressions and evidence preparation can
proceed in parallel once their own reviewed scope and real prerequisites are
met. This register grants no implementation, resource release, native execution,
rollout or reassignment.
