0.694. Whole-contract reading supports a shared presentation/provider theme,
**not seventeen duplicates, a single defect, or seventeen missing patches**.

## Typed dependencies and rejected shortcuts

1. **Authority -> consumption, not equivalence:** #5555 supplies the
   owning-facts boundary used by #6832/#8490. #4183 adds shared bidirectional
   requests, not merely another name for the read-side feed. Do not infer
   all-pivot acceptance from a Worktrees-only correction.
2. **Identity -> selected rendering/actions:** #4118's producer provenance
   can support #3657's joins; #2801 and #4182 consume different task versus
   child-lineage baselines. Taxonomy, liveness and display are not custody.
3. **Composition -> front-door coherence:** #1193's absent-contribution
   behavior intersects #542's guided first run. Neither receives the other's
   whole outcome; #1239's credited transplant is a prerequisite milestone,
   not a reason to restore retired fallback or close assigned sources.
4. **Responsiveness is not one defect:** #7939's modal/source/epoch fences,
   #8490's aggregate/reload scheduling, #6832's startup recovery and #4274's
   optional progress richness need their own evidence. The standing numeric
   performance programme does not silently become every issue's native gate.
5. **Graceful versus abrupt ownership:** #4225's unexpected-exit backstop
   is separate from ordinary cancellation. #5898's durable attach plan and
   #4117's no-effect preview are not permission to reassign or clean resources.

## Completed-work matching and disposition

The published bounded retrieval supplies three PR/commit candidates per
member, not whole-history coverage. This study retains those candidates at
their original description/message/documentary tiers. Documentary matches
cannot demonstrate delivered source behavior; source messages and claimed
merges cannot demonstrate native/installed acceptance.

The issue discussions explicitly preserve useful completed credit, especially
#1239's technical milestones and Worktrees-only feed/Actions/source repairs,
but also assigned-source, claimed integration, pre-mount correction and
installed gates. None is independently accepted as whole completion here.

for these families, using existing phase owners and open bounded-source
coordination. Before any separate disposition, refresh the exact source and
receiving metadata/text where changed, verify complete actual receiving
Plan/local and global Validation/Coordination/Journal delivery, and honor each

register is not that acceptance. Every source remains OPEN and every existing
owner, hold, release/installed duty and parent completion gate remains.
