mechanical coverage. Their existence does not establish universal invocation,
lifecycle or installed behavioral conformance. The remaining finite contract
must not acquire completion of every unrelated #1096 item as a new gate.

The original asks for a complete runtime-agent authoring shape, not one
filesystem layout, command prefix, service-unit spelling or manifest schema.
Its required/optional distinction and **validation/checklist command OR
equivalent CI checks**, rejecting **incomplete OR ambiguous** plugins
**before release**, remain literal alternatives and timing.

This authoring contract applies to runtime `agent-*` plugins at their supported
scope. It does not extend installation cells beyond the suite-private
runtime-bearing identities already eligible for them. Payload-only and
unrelated plugins remain outside cell namespacing. Supported ordinary non-cell
operation and optional coexistence remain valid.
