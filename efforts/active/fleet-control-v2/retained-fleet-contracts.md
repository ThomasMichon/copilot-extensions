## Coordination and retained charter

Keep the current Plan steward, contract implementer, Gateway/index integrator,
existing campaign owners and adopter roles. #5862's isolated
observation/store/reader reservation does not acquire #4121 reconciliation or
transcript powers. Original worktree/task/delegate/native transcript owners
retain those operations; #3761 keeps origin/write-through authority and #352 its
UX/mux source. Authentication/routing #5789/#6443, client installer #6424/#6432,
deployment #6030 and existing effect/resource owners retain their source.

One fleet belongs to one operator; future collaboration is fleet-to-fleet. The
parent's two authority/account-domain checks do not authorize a second operator's
state/credentials in one fleet. The NEW Gateway scope does not take over older
adopter Gateways.

Retain current source appointments and literal whole-acceptance/interface
conditions. Generic Taking or a historical bounded milestone does not release a
source claim, and product allocation retirement is not a tracker-claim release.
Concrete source/DTO/current-policy ownership must be agreed before affected
runtime wiring; silence is not consent. This intake creates no universal ACK
tour, contact requirement, new implementer or reservation expansion.

Support both release-verification methods through an explicitly selected
required installation policy, not automatic fallback or two mandatory proofs on
every release. The GitHub-positive canonical-manifest gate remains deferred
under its existing authorization constraint; this amendment authorizes no
ceremony, signing workflow or trust root.

The already-appointed fixed `manager.converge`/single deployment-reference and
same-journal named-resource receipt seam remain narrow #6241 contributions.
They are not a takeover of general operation ownership or whole
manager/operation completion. No separate ungranted issue is absorbed.
