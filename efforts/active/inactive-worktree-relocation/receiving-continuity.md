# Qualified owner observation and receiving continuity

[Canonical Active effort](README.md) · [Umbrella #10879](https://github.com/ThomasMichon/copilot-extensions/issues/10879)

## Scope and status

This is a scoped prerequisite proposal, not implemented support. It continues
the original support → old merged/final practice → broader inactive sequence.
The decomposition below is **agent-recommended realization**. Actual adoption,
installation, owner recovery and target effects remain separately owned.

The first extension targets a completed clean, sessionless, unpaired ordinary
linked checkout carrying **valid retained receiving-allocation and creator
proof**. Keep worktree identity, branch/HEAD/index/content, original allocation
and creation history, claims, lifecycle and execution/Git-family authority.
No active origin dependency, unsettled obligation, pair, WIP, retained session,
opaque backend, corrupt/foreign/ambiguous receipt or unknown liveness is admitted.

## Source-confirmed prerequisite boundary

- `checkout_relocation._ordinary_shape` excludes receiving allocations;
  `_ordinary` also excludes retained creation receipts. Rehydrating a missing
  field does not make that class supported.
- `execution_spaces.require_record_mutation(..., recover_receiving=False)`
  refuses missing receiving metadata on an owner-ref/nonce record. Read-only
  planning must retain that refusal; it must not migrate an index, save a
  sidecar, rewrite a receipt or finish a pending creation.
- `receiving_allocation.require_record` and
  `receiving_creation_journal.concluded_identity` bind the original journaled
  allocation, nonce, branch, creator and path. A new checkout location cannot
  be authorized by overwriting those historical identities.
- `sessions.verify_worktree_active` retains failed/unavailable probes through
  `probes_ok`. On Windows its strict path may execute PSMux `has-session`, but
  nonzero results cannot prove absence. Finality, no recorded sessions, PID
  absence, a healthy resident or a fresh roster are not substitutions.

The [published preservation boundary](../../../plugins/agent-worktrees/docs/mux.md#preserve-first-when-discovery-is-unavailable)
from #11274 explicitly states that these owners have no qualified non-mutating
PSMux observation adapter. Nominal CLI reads can perform startup registration
cleanup. Strict action-time checks are not claimed passive or harmless.
Consequently an existing planning path that invokes that check must not be
treated as a side-effect-free mux inspection merely because apply is false.

The separate open roster-freshness work in #11433 preserves publication
quality and existing owners; it is not a complete native mux-absence contract.
Do not pre-patch that contributor's work or promote missing mux provenance
into an authoritative negative.

## Dependency-ordered source outcomes

1. **Qualify the observation owner.** Acquire a supported non-mutating native
   mux observation that proves its actual producer/server incarnation,
   execution-space scope, completeness and freshness, including relevant
   unmanaged/unknown hosting. Preserve bound-process and launch/lifecycle
   fences. Until then, unknown remains refusal. Do not reconstruct a backend
   reader from arbitrary registrations/protocols or issue unsafe CLI probes.
   Read-only relocation planning must not invoke an unqualified effectful
   observation path.
2. **Inspect or reconcile original proof explicitly.** Use the existing
   allocation/creation owners and original request, not replacement allocation
   or default-derived paths. A source-owned reconciliation may be a separate
   explicit operation; pure planning never performs it. Missing, malformed,
   foreign, ambiguous or unsupported original evidence stays blocked.
3. **Evolve only current location.** Under the existing serialized receiving,
   creation, tracking, lifecycle and relocation owners, introduce a durable
   current-location revision bound to the unchanged original identity and
   operation. Prove original concluded child state and settled creator
   responsibility before admission. Do not rewrite allocation receipts,
   terminal conclusions, creation coordinates or parent closeout history.
4. **Complete the first real flow.** Exercise native copy/publication, Git
   repair, pointer/permission rebinding, actual mutation/publication and
   lifecycle/allocation-reader consumers. The original creator's immutable
   allocation identity and original creation result remain explainable after
   movement. Qualify stale-writer/version behavior; unsupported readers refuse
   rather than erase provenance or roll location backward.
5. **Promote only actual qualification.** Use normal review, required CI and
   accepted Main publication. Installation and real target/cohort effects do
   not follow from a plan, source merge or synthetic pass.

## Existing owner operations are not pure plan reads

`worktree-allocation inspect --owner-ref <origin> --json` consumes a bounded
original token/request selector through the origin's registered RMW owner.
`worktree-allocation-receive inspect --json` consumes the original receiving
envelope in the selected project. These front doors may acquire/create
operation locks or sidecars. Normal receiving authority can recover omitted
metadata, upgrade a legacy creation index, retain a conclusion, or recover a
pending original tail; pending recovery may complete original companion
composition. They are not substitutes for effect-free planning.

`worktree-allocation reconcile --owner-ref <origin> --json` can contact the
receiving owner and advance bind/confirm/closed responsibility based on
original proof. It preserves frozen-owner, ambiguity, handoff and unknown
effect refusals. Do not force-clear claims, fabricate conclusions or select an
unrelated status write merely to rehydrate a receipt.

## Qualification and refusal matrix

- Read-only plan: prove no receipt/index/sidecar/history write and no unsafe
  mux command, including missing proof and owner unavailability.
- First positive case: actual ordinary linked Git checkout, clean locally
  landed content, valid original receiving journal/receipt, concluded child,
  settled creator responsibility and qualified inactive owners.
- Refusals: active parent dependency, unconfirmed owner, stale/foreign tuple,
  changed nonce/branch/receipt, ambiguous original request, pending creation,
  opaque obligations, pair/session/WIP, unsafe storage or permission drift.
- Interruptions: before/after location revision and ledger publication,
  Git repair, destination publication and source retirement. Retain original
  journal/stage/copies and recover the same operation; abort remains
  prepublication only. No replacement allocation or rewritten evidence.
- Consumer/version checks: original allocation inspection/conclusion, record
  mutation, source publication, launch admission and stale generic writers.
  Installed/resident selection is separately qualified through its owner.

**Current blocker:** the qualified native observation contract is absent.
No receiving-class runtime implementation or native positive qualification is
claimed by this proposal. Preserve the existing holds and source worktree;
do not manufacture a passing move through mocked liveness or a recovered flag.
