receiving creation owner, settles its actual creator obligation, transfers and
repairs real Git/filesystem state, and exercises record mutation, original
inspection, cancellation and interrupted recovery. Positive admission is scoped
only to the newly owned fixture identities; native owner probes are poisoned.
It supplies no producer claiming complete real-host inactivity.

controlled pull-forward, including prepublication cancellation, interrupted

physical prior runtime. That reader accepts original-location records, refuses

This is a focused component packet, not full native inactivity qualification.
