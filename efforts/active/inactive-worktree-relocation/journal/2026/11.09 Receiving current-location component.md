## Scope reconciliation

Continue the existing #10879 effort and #11483's reviewed receiving-continuity
intent. This realizes part of the Worktrees vision's identity-preserving
relocation and replay-safe allocation guarantees through their existing
owners; it does not extend the vision or introduce another allocator/service.
Independent receiving-component qualification is not complete native-owner
observation or permission to operate on retained adopter worktrees.

The first implementation deliberately supports a finalized, clean, sessionless,
unpaired receiving allocation with intact original proof and an exactly closed,
released creator obligation in the same project and execution space. Missing,
foreign, ambiguous, unsettled or unsupported responsibility remains refused.
Additional receiving revisions, original missing-proof reconciliation,
WAL-backed proof, retained sessions, pairs and WIP remain open.

  retain original-location support and refuse the distinct receiving journal
  schema before mutation, including generic stale writes and cancellation.
  Existing ordinary relocation journals and writer generation are unchanged.

## Focused qualification

The receiving-only native packet creates the original child through the actual
receiving creation owner, settles its actual creator obligation, transfers and
repairs real Git/filesystem state, and exercises record mutation, original
inspection, cancellation and interrupted recovery. Positive admission is scoped
only to the newly owned fixture identities; native owner probes are poisoned.
It supplies no producer claiming complete real-host inactivity.

The exact committed receiving/pure-plan packet passed 38 controls after a
controlled pull-forward, including prepublication cancellation, interrupted
recovery, missing/corrupt original and location-proof fences, and a selected
physical prior runtime. That reader accepts original-location records, refuses
the unfamiliar receiving schema, and leaves fixture identity/state unchanged.
Imported source-byte checks bind the receiving consumer to the selected source.
This is a focused component packet, not full native inactivity qualification.

Normal review, required exact-source CI and ordinary accepted Main publication
remain independent delivery gates. No installation, runtime/service/default
change or real target operation follows. Keep this effort and #10879 Active
through complete native-owner, receiving-flow, mixed-root and adopter acceptance.
