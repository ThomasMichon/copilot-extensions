## Scope and design

This is the five-clause #2633 correction retained directly in #2830 and in
P2830-6..10/V2830-2, following reviewed plan #9496. The resolution watchdog
must bound project/configuration/plan acquisition, including the direct
fallback's own resolution and refresh loop. It must not bound the lifetime
of a successfully handed-off local executable or interactive SSH child.

The direct fallback accepts an optional cancellation callback and invokes it
after child creation, immediately before the existing wait helper enters its
normal or interrupt-cleanup wait. The launcher retains unconditional timer
cancellation in `finally`, including resolution failures and `SystemExit`.
No timeout, reaper, ancestry, termination authority or session-lifetime policy
changes.

`cmd_launch` resides with its existing launch-owner helpers in
`manager_launch_cli`; the root CLI retains its compatibility re-export and
late-bound callback/configuration seams. Whole-file strict checking requires
accurate annotations across the touched legacy files. Lazy compatibility
names are declared from their actual first-party owners without eager runtime
loading, blanket module stubs, checker suppression or project admission.
