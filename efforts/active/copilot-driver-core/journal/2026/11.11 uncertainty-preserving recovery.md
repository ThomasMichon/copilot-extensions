# Uncertainty-preserving crash recovery proposed

Back to the [canonical effort](../../README.md).

Prepared a recovery-boundary amendment for abrupt disconnect, process loss
and host or observer restart. A qualified clean SDK observer boundary may add
evidence, but is not a prerequisite for the new crash path. Prior clean-path
negative results and refusal behavior remain unchanged; missing terminal
events never become a fabricated completion certificate.

The original lifecycle owner retains durable transition and admission state;
the receiver's first leaf is candidate/status export and a strict reducer
through that owner, with effects unwired. Platform custody stays with its
existing providers. Whole-scope settlement, current authorization, same
conversation, no replay and explicit uncertain-work reconciliation remain
required. No observer, credential, native process or deployment effect is
authorized, and no incomplete parent aggregate is marked complete.

The receiver's actor-free leaf landed in #12144 as
`9219860f3023b6a37c49dd7ede9b8fe1ca86d0d7`, after exact-head approval and
green required CI. Its strict reducer retains uncertainty and ordered
transitions through an injected store protocol, with no production adapter or
native callbacks. This is not a settlement certificate or activation.

The next retained dependency is the original-owner journal/admission adapter,
initially using the existing Worktrees guarded writer and launch-state
namespace. A full-record codec and passive read projection are a disjoint
receiver-owned source leaf. No new store, supervisor, process policy or
platform custody transfer is proposed.
