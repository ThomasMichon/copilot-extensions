## Desktop observer evidence hardening

Read-only native diagnosis could not causally attribute the historical coverage
gap or ambiguous foreground transitions. The fixture now separates raw
foreground HWND/PID/TID and visible-window capture from costly process census
and lineage enrichment. It supplements polling with registering-thread
out-of-context WinEvent hooks, retains chronological raw evidence and capture
brackets, requires readiness before fixture launch and seals the report only
after final capture, drain, checked unhook and join. NULL, missing enumeration,

well as callback, coverage and immutable-report controls. A separate explicit

qualification remain open. No production enrollment or runtime rollout is
implied by these source changes.
