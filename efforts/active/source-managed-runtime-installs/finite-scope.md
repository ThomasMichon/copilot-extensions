## Request and finite boundary

The [original request](inception-transcript.md#initial-request) separates service
deployment from Copilot content delivery and selects clean merged source,
immutable generations and independent source policy. The
[full transition request](inception-transcript.md#full-transition-and-recurring-maintenance)
includes all installed services/tools and regular source maintenance. The
[same-installation clarification](inception-transcript.md#wholesale-same-installation-cutover)
requires existing global commands, configuration and databases, not a new cell
or copied data.

Retain the actual later requirements: promotion-mediated UV/wheel inputs,
per-app staging/activation, Git-addressed reuse without local version increments,
incoming-first ordinary/repair replacement, automatic-only inhibition, the
independent foundation and the public force-fresh entry. The
[inception record](inception-transcript.md) and unchanged Guiding Intent remain
authoritative; implementation choices and this consolidation are recommendations.

Use one explicitly declared installed inventory, selected roles/profiles,
installation/source route and recovery choice for the finite acceptance record.
Preserve source-policy support for Main, stable/exact pins and opted-in Dev,
but do not require Main or older-pin execution on an adopter unless its chosen
installation/recovery route needs it. Source capability support and adopter
selection are different facts.

Regular-maintenance acceptance is one unchanged regular cycle with reuse and
one advancing regular cycle through the canonical installer, with applicable
existing failure, consent, native lifetime and single-owner evidence. Ongoing
product operation is the outcome, not a requirement to observe it indefinitely.

  [shared YAML banner](maintenance-banner.md) consumer/standby implementation.
- #9048 and existing #8669/#8864/session/removal owners retain the
  [critical-connectivity](connectivity-retention.md) and
  [installation-independent-session](../installation-independent-sessions/README.md)
  acceptance.
- #5353 retains historical combined-trigger causal qualification under its
  nested/source/slot/routing custodians.

In particular, an adopter may explicitly require its original Copilot/Mux/session
substrate and both current and fresh authenticated SSH connections to survive
removal of all supporting code, together with native thirty-minute maintenance
standby. Those remain real adopter/removal-owner obligations, not withdrawn
goals, technical passes or resources presumed gone. Preserve the relevant source
prerequisites and obtain the existing owner's actual acceptance before claiming
that adoption milestone. Generic source qualification alone cannot close it.

Preserve every source/resource reservation, protected process, native venue,
attempt/budget limit and rollout approval. An open issue, a received message,
the age of a claim or a reviewed plan is not custody transfer or permission to
execute. No live cleanup, retirement, configuration change or release follows
from this document.
