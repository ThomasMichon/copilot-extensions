## Source-process observations, not native-frame acceptance

The warm 120x40 fixture retains 40 complete samples per stage, with raw
monotonic wall/current-process CPU/RSS values and nearest-rank p50/p95/p99/max
in the `sidecar-scale-observation` JUnit property. The final report also
identifies the exact four source-file hashes, Python/OS and cache conditions.
Strip caches are cleared before each visible paint; source/app state is warm.
The before/after runs use Linux, Python 3.13.12 and Textual 8.2.8.

| Observed stage | Before p95 | Final p95 | Final p99/max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Uncached visible painting | 4.76 ms | 2.60 ms | 2.91 ms |
| Live delta through Textual Pilot idle | 405.94 ms | 77.92 ms | 104.50 ms |

Final median current-process CPU is 2.04 ms for painting and 30.86 ms for a
delta-to-idle sample. Sampled whole-pytest RSS is approximately 71.10-71.57 MB;
it is not isolated widget allocation or child-memory coverage. Child CPU is
not measured. Earlier optimized observations are retained separately rather
than discarded to select the final result.

These are source-fixture stages, not executable-to-visible, native key
acknowledgment or complete native-frame latency. Small-sample source tails,
installed performance and the complete repaint target remain unqualified.
No wall-clock threshold is promoted into required CI.
