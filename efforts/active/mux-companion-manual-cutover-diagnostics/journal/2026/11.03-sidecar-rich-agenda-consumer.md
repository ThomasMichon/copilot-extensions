## Implemented boundaries

- The selected engine interpreter must declare and actually parse passive
  `worktree-data-v1` before dispatch. Store-filtered inventory must identify
  exactly one registered agenda home with matching owner, worktree, incarnation
  and placement fences. Later reads retain and recheck that placement; an
  allocation descriptor alone never validates agenda contents.
- The shared activation resolver selects one enabled efforts payload for the
  current checkout. Manager invokes its declared rich reader in an isolated
  child rather than importing a sibling runtime, guessing a home, accepting
  publisher-supplied argv or starting an absent service.
- The owning reader must match the published store/worktree. Its accepted
  revision cannot precede publication or retained evidence. Equal revisions
  agree on acceptance time. Reader-ahead evidence remains distinct from
  delivered current projections.
- Immutable operation IDs, canonical cursors, original acceptance timestamps,
  reported historical actors and explicit payload omissions reach the virtual
  timeline. Accepted planning history remains partial evidence, not proof of
  physical execution, authenticated callers, current head custody or archival
  completeness.
- One bounded coalescing task consumes existing subscription callbacks. Rich
  latest/history reads exist only while Timeline has demand; backward pages
  keep independent live cursors. At most four pages bridge a coalesced jump.
  Larger gaps refuse with a fresh-viewer recovery path, never invented history.
- Unavailable-source recovery validates a fresh latest snapshot and exact
  bounded replay atomically in the viewer. A failed later chunk cannot leave
  partial events or old facts reactivated. Immutable-content and proof-budget
  fences survive recovery. Only this exceptional path copies the already
  bounded viewer window; ordinary pushes do not clone it.
- Canonical head invalidation clears active rows immediately, even while a rich
  point read is blocked. Hide, reload and unmount join owned cancellation.
  Late callbacks cannot change newer selections. Failed retirement retains its
  terminal custody and prevents replacement reads.
