This is an agent-recommended planned recovery case under #10377, not an
implementation, installed qualification, process claim or permission to
update a host. The seven technical checks live only in the ROOT Plan and
Validation Plan. Existing Dispatch supervisor, installer, completed-cycle,
capture/restoration, native-process and worker owners retain their scope.

## Evidence and gap

The qualified original reports a Windows Dispatch update whose incoming
coordinator became healthy at `0.20.1-dev1`, while post-install supervisor
reconciliation failed at `master_observation`: the retained
`0.19.11-dev1` master lacked completed-cycle support and an acceptable
prepared record. The outer durable-launch error did not establish a Job,
privilege, WMI or process-launch failure.

The subsequent qualified discussion reports a successful explicit
coordinator-only preservation update to `0.20.6-dev1`. Its native incoming
coordinator answered after the installer exited; supervisor launcher
definitions, all original runtime directories, original master-prefix bytes
and the original master's native identity were preserved. This is reported
source evidence, not a new execution or independent live verification.
It does not qualify the legacy master's capability or migration, or a
successful ordinary full update. Optional retained-restoration capture is
also still separate; #13667's planned path-spelling intake is not a fix.

Keep three outcomes distinct: incoming coordinator completion, the
supervisor-master transition/post-install result, and optional retained
capture/restoration. A clear update queue, matching version/deploy metadata
or healthy coordinator alone cannot accept all three.

## P13617-1 - Attribute the actual incomplete boundary

Through the existing installation and supervisor owners, identify the
affected legacy/current pair, actual selected incoming runtime, original
master generation/start token/native identity, capability observation and
prepared-record status. Preserve the original failure and distinguish
absence, unsupported capability, unreadable/unknown evidence and real
incoming failure. A running process is not completed-cycle readiness.

Keep the inner refusal attributable instead of diagnosing it as failure to
escape an installer Job. Preserve the already-qualified coordinator-only
mode and its reported limits; neither it nor a later version-current skip
may rewrite the earlier incomplete full-update result as success.

## P13617-2 - Supported transition OR actionable prerequisite

Provide a supported owner-controlled upgrade/handoff for the affected pair
OR an actionable explicit prerequisite that preserves existing workers,
accepted operations and native custody. Keep that original alternative;
this plan neither selects an implementation nor requires both routes.
Reconcile the selected route with the existing
[authoritative replacement contract](../../../visions/installer/README.md#authoritative-runtime-replacement):
old completed-cycle/recovery support cannot become permission for incoming
replacement, and actual incoming readiness, state compatibility and complete
current selection still define success.

Preserve singleton, start-token, native-instance and existing prepared-record
checks at their owning boundaries. Do not remove checks, manufacture
status/preparation, clear the singleton, start a duplicate active master or
force-kill an owner/tree to obtain this case's evidence. Existing protected
workers, session holders and connectivity are not central-cleanup targets.
The broader replacement owner's role/generation-scoped policy remains with
that owner; this documentary case grants no process effect or new veto.

Name any real incoming authentication, permission, state-compatibility, I/O
or health prerequisite honestly. An unsupported legacy protocol alone is not
a new manual recovery ceremony or an indefinite incoming-selection fence.
If the whole requested transition is unqualified, retain that gap without
calling the coordinator-only result a complete service update.

## P13617-3 - Preserve repeat and post-install truth

Carry the uncompleted supervisor-profile result through the existing
installer/result owner. A subsequent same-version/current shortcut must
not erase or silently skip it, advertise successful full convergence, or
replay an uncertain prior operation. Reconcile actual prior effects before
another transition, keeping coordinator completion and optional capture
independently observable.

Keep #11875's earlier generated-launcher sharing failure and its canonical
repair separate from this later capability boundary. #12187/#12217's
disposable source/native qualification is useful prior credit, not a
qualified live transition of this retained master.

## V13617-1 - Actual legacy/current transition

At the original Windows consumer boundary, retain actual legacy/current
process identities and completed-cycle evidence while qualifying the
selected supported transition OR prerequisite. Cover missing capability and
missing/mismatched preparation, a valid supported positive, and unknown or
failed evidence without fabricated readiness, duplicate masters, lost
workers/accepted operations or collateral process actions. Record actual
source/release/native inputs and unavailable coverage separately; units or a
healthy coordinator do not substitute for this requested native transition.

## V13617-2 - Explicit preservation mode

Retain a distinct coordinator-only positive: incoming physical runtime and
health after installer exit, unchanged supervisor launcher definitions,
preserved original runtime directories, unchanged original master-prefix
bytes and the same original master native identity. Do not infer the
master's readiness, ordinary reconciliation or full-update completion.
Reuse already-qualified preservation evidence where its actual inputs and
coverage apply; this does not reopen that result or request another update.
Preservation qualification does not authorize ordinary restart fallback,
supervisor reconciliation or a production retry.

## V13617-3 - Failure and immediate-repeat reporting

Exercise the normal full-update/post-install result and subsequent
version-current entry against the uncompleted profile gate. Preserve the
inner cause and earlier partial result; no successful skip may hide the
remaining transition. Keep actual incoming failures failed, unknown effects
unreplayed and capture/restoration separately unqualified where applicable.
