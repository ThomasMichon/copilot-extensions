## Observed gap and bounded outcome

The explicit-body qualification in #11197 proves that existing submission keeps
prepared bytes through native serialization, durable receipt retention and
confirmed retries. It does not record whether an opaque body was admitted as
shared preparation, retain structured comment/write identity, or make every
publisher invoke preparation. Parsing a copied marker from an arbitrary legacy
body cannot supply that missing admission fact.

The next finite slice binds the shared preparation's minimal identity and exact
body to the selected action in the **existing** protected intent/receipt owner.
It then projects intended comment/write identity to that owner's original native
receipt without treating a body marker as authorship, current-version readback,
feedback echo, handled progress or permission to send again.

## Ownership and admission

Keep the actual AI profile and Worktrees source owners authoritative for
preparation. Compose before final preview/approval and immutable intent
admission; never decorate or allocate identities in the executor after selection.
The submitting producer retains the public GUIDs, exact bytes and original
source snapshot through its existing selected-operation custody.

An admitted prepared action carries only the necessary structured identity and
body binding alongside its existing body. Do not retain a second body copy,
profile history, transcript or parallel allocation database. The body and
structured identity must agree with the shared preparer's actual contract.
Caller labels do not establish audience policy, human review or producer source.
An edit retains logical comment identity and changes intentional-write identity;
a new reply does not reuse its parent's identities.

The existing operation/action identity remains the retry key. Reusing an
admitted operation with different body, GUIDs or source refuses. Restart,
handoff, lost response and later profile changes retain the original prepared
intent; recovery cannot redecorate it or allocate a replacement write.
Keep existing native target, actor, source/candidate and owned-edit checks.

## Strict storage and peer compatibility

The current review-intent/v1 action shapes reject extra fields. Extend the
owning selected-intent and durable envelope contracts explicitly before
admitting new prepared-action metadata. Use the existing lifecycle owner's
reader transition and transaction/capacity reservation; a request cannot
silently upgrade disk state or strand old readers.

Legacy admitted operations, unresolved attempts and receipts stay readable and
unchanged. They do not gain preparation evidence from current profiles or a
marker. Existing opaque-body/default operations remain supported at their
current semantics, not falsely advertised as mandatory compact stamping.

Native/service/tool consumers select the executing receiver's supported
prepared-intent contract before sending new shapes. Keep strict old readers,
clients and parsers strict. A missing helper, unsupported receiver or rollback
reports unavailable for the new path, not a legacy send that claims preparation.
Do not introduce a preflight solely where the existing operation can consume
backward-compatible information; negotiate only the actual changed boundary.
Rolling back source does not downgrade persisted state or erase unknown effects.

## Receipt projection, not marker discovery

Read through the actual existing scoped owner. Bind a projection to the exact
operation/action and admitted prepared identity, native endpoint/account/repo,
PR/thread/comment, native principal and retained receipt/version evidence.
An unknown or unattempted action remains unknown or unavailable, preserving any
known partial native IDs. Equal content, a GUID match, copied tags or a current
vote cannot upgrade it into confirmation.

Distinguish confirmed original native delivery from fresh native readback and
interest-specific echo qualification. Existing B3/B4 capture and original-version
proof remain authoritative; a new correlation projection cannot advance their
frontiers, ACK a conversation, emit Dispatch or merge native objects. Keep
unsupported reply/edit/vote version attribution and longer echo chains open.

Retain only bounded identity/receipt metadata under the current retention owner.
Terminal compaction must preserve unresolved attempts and the existing keeper
requirements; this slice authorizes no deletion or new body archive.
