## Reproducible read-only consumer flow

Use the [supported repository-local profile](../../../../../services/pr/LOCAL.md)
with an authorized Gitea target, admitted observation credentials and an
accountable hosting owner:

1. Construct and install the closed application through the host's supported
   lifecycle. Confirm the selected native/service contract, passive readiness
   and actual running owner after the installation caller exits.
2. Query through the installed local CLI, not a direct database read.
   Wait for complete bounded inventory and populated current candidate/review
   facts. The observed inventory completes in two pages; departed historical
   entries remain explicitly nonmembers rather than current PRs.
3. Register a finite continuing reader from the current cursor. Consume
   status, PR-list and next-action frames through the UID-bound local ingress.
   Reconnect using the original stream cursor and confirm the same registration,
   expiry and independent ACK position. These reads must not initiate forge
   acquisition.
4. Let the existing ordinary observation cycle discover a naturally arriving
   PR. Consume its actual entry/baseline events after reconnecting from the
   earlier cursor. Delivery advances independently of ACK; explicitly
   acknowledge the delivered event and verify the original registration and
   expiry remain unchanged.
5. Close the finite stream connections. The continuing service remains under
   its existing lifecycle authority; the finite verification reader expires
   naturally. Do not start another consumer loop or modify the observation
   budget to manufacture the exit.
