## Local source boundary

This is the first runtime expansion following reviewed #13310, not completion
of the historical transition or P/V/G5947-3/4. The outer version-2 receiver
accepts only active state version 1 (the existing legacy list) and executor
admission epoch 1. Its existing writer preserves opaque unselected partitions,
selected extensions and the recorded epoch, even after the last subscription
is removed. There is no envelope publisher, migration command, active version-2
map, cwd capability or caller-default change.

The generation is a source-owned, monotonically ordered admission epoch of
the actual executing implementation, separate from package/build provenance.
An incompatible future epoch must advance this constant in its own code.
It is not a total ordering of package releases, a selection stamp, or an
environment override. Existing governed invocation validation still establishes
the actual immutable interpreter/module slot. Epoch 1 cannot restart against
epoch 2, even if an old retained partition would otherwise be readable.

Cold legacy serve and self-boot check before resident admission/spawn; restart
checks before acquiring custody or requesting predecessor shutdown. Governed
ensure checks before starting its transaction and again under that guard.
Writer adoption already reads through the final native lease, and activation/
resume now rechecks disk admission before effects. Passive construction remains
effect-free; it does not become an active owner without adoption/activation.
This is state-executor admission, not an installation-readiness or
current-selection veto.

The process-local atomic lock now spans the final read/modify/write. The final
byte recheck refuses a concurrently changed document before replacement;
another partition cannot be overwritten by a stale pre-serialization snapshot.
Existing definite-failure and uncertain-durability behavior remain distinct.
The native writer lease remains necessary; this does not add a competing
cross-process lock.

## Repeated-write cost

The writer adds one initial full-document read/parse before preparing a
replacement, in addition to the prior final-boundary read. The lock spans
that read, serialization and replacement. This is necessary to preserve
unselected partitions and reject delayed snapshots; an earlier cached
document cannot authorize a final write. There are no extra processes,
pollers, network calls or persistent history records.

On one shared Windows host, 100 alternating warm samples per arm and size
completed without errors (600 total writes). At 10 legacy entries, prior/new
list p50 was 5.91/6.97 ms and nearest-rank p95 was 21.88/15.69 ms; the new
versioned arm was 7.35/25.22 ms. At 1,000 entries, prior/new list p50 was
43.91/58.66 ms and p95 was 90.24/126.95 ms; the versioned arm was
60.02/130.60 ms. Complete samples, means/maxima, source digests and opaque-state
assertions are retained with the owning qualification packet. These samples
do not establish p99, fleet tails or a dedicated-host latency budget.

## Retained gates

Unaware historical inline executables do not acquire epoch-awareness from
this new source. Their old whole-file address remains unsafe for publishing
the authoritative envelope. The existing owner still needs reviewed,
recoverable physical isolation/migration, preservation of pending/queued/retry
identity, prevention of duplicate legacy replay and lost transition-time
registrations, and historical cold/self-boot/rollback qualification.
No new versioned state is emitted before that work passes.

Next: establish that guarded transition under the existing storage/lifecycle
owner, then adapt and qualify the retained per-subscription map/cwd prototype,
then migrate callers separately. Do not silently drop the historical family,
enable context writes from this receiver-only result, or infer installation,
service-stop, rollout, credential or retained-resource authority. The parent
remains Active.
