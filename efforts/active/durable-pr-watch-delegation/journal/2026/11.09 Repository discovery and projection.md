# Configured repository discovery and durable projection

Part of #11783 and the [reviewed M1 contract](../../repository-cache-events-m1.md),
under active parent #6007. This is stage 2 source work, not local-front-door
or live acceptance.

The existing ReplayObserver now discovers configured GitHub/Gitea populations
without registrations and unions their account/backend-qualified targets with
existing waits. It reuses the four-reader executor and native Worktrees bridge.
Configured-only reads are capped at 32 per cycle with rotation; legacy waits
keep their existing admission. Native inventory keeps explicit selected config,
credential argv and finite page/member/time limits. Snapshots carry that same
explicit native configuration, and mismatched provider/endpoint facts cannot
replace the last confirmed state.

RepositoryProjection reuses ReplayStore's existing owner envelope/operation
transaction, not a second file/database writer. Current normalized facts and
durable typed events replace atomically. Partial/outage inventory retains known
members; complete omission is only selection departure until bounded native
reads establish closure/merge. Initial unavailable differs from empty complete,
and recovery of never-populated inventory supplies baseline rather than later
entry. Head, review and check-context changes are semantic; timestamp/cursor-only
refreshes are not. Strict normalized storage refuses corrupt or foreign facts.

Qualification through the bounded supervisor/disconnected container:

- 162 discovery/schema/native-bridge/legacy-observer controls pass, with two
  existing platform-specific skips.
- The final 15-case discovery selection passes after adding post-replace
  uncertainty and initial-outage/empty-complete controls.
- Authoritative merged facts are terminal even when the provider's open
  selection/lifecycle field has not yet converged; the native fields themselves
  are preserved rather than rewritten.
- Both provider populations populate through real owned synthetic native
  children without registered waits. Repeated cache queries add no forge reads;
  overlapping waits share acquisition. Rotation retains unread known membership.
- Before-replace failure preserves old state/events; after-replace uncertainty
  restores current facts and the same event IDs together without duplicate
  semantic publication on retry.

The actual native source trace exposed a coupled resource gap: typed snapshot
review pages could keep returning full pages indefinitely, and GitHub's selected
transport still used legacy unbounded capture. The existing read-credential
owner now charges a 64-request native snapshot budget; typed GitHub/Gitea capture
remains byte-bounded, while ordinary legacy and unrelated providers are
unchanged. Executing native declaration fields carry those bounds and selected
repository observation refuses prior receivers missing them. The 335-case
Worktrees credential/inventory/provider selection passes, including exact
exhaustion of nonterminating pages for both providers. This is a finite-source
bound, not a measured real-target traffic allowance or release/installation.

Cleanup/authority loss is not normalized into a settled read failure.

This is correction evidence; merge qualification follows a new independent
