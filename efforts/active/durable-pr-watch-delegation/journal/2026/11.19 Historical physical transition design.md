## Evidence and accepted source scope

The actual `24b79724ac` lease opens its legacy path before acquiring the
native nonblocking lock. Its ordinary CLI constructs the daemon only after
that acquisition. A newer launcher does not intercept its own-module
self-boot; the existing installation transaction does not constrain that
historical entry.

A contained Windows fixture pauses the historical lease after open and
before its unchanged lock operation. Rename refuses with sharing violation
32. The child then acquires/releases normally and exits zero. After moving
the closed lock and creating a directory at its old path, another historical
lease open refuses with `PermissionError`. No daemon, subscription migration,
provider read or callback is performed. The source hash matches the retained
historical lease used in the earlier mutual-exclusion audit. This is exact
lease-operation evidence only, not a complete historical installation or
crash-safe physical transition.

The POSIX retained-descriptor race is source/OS-semantics analysis; no native
Linux passing result is claimed. The operator accepted the one-time POSIX
historical-migration boot boundary as **source design**: seal the historical
path, keep legacy state authoritative, and wait for an actual subsequent
kernel boot before isolated activation. Windows may avoid that wait only
after its sharing/competing-start behavior is qualified. The
[complete question and submitted answer](../../subscriber-callback-context.md#physical-transition-feasibility-and-accepted-migration-choice)
are preserved in the canonical amendment.

## Next source boundary

Take the concrete physical-transition amendment through normal review and
CI before runtime realization. Reuse the existing boot-identity reader from
the already-declared update-driver dependency, writer and persistence
owners. Preserve one authority through preparation, sealing, boot wait,
final import and active recovery; exercise every interrupted boundary.

The first review identified that POSIX move-then-mkdir can split delayed and
cold callers across lock inodes if the controller exits in the gap. The
proposal now requires an uninterrupted atomic exchange under the original
lease, forbids that fallback, and names the exact two-caller/controller-exit
native control. Windows recovery may not turn its retained closed inode into
a second lease during a raced preparation. These are design requirements,
not claims that either transition backend has been implemented or qualified.

No map/cwd capability, caller default, live migration, reboot, installation,
service stop, rollout or resource cleanup follows from this design decision.
P/V/G5947-3/4 and the broader parent remain open. The provider prototype and
all retained external obligations remain separate and preserved.
