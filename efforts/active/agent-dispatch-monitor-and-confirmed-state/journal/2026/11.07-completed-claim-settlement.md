# Completed-claim settlement before workspace conclusion

Component of [plan-assignment-startup](../../plan-assignment-startup.md).

## Gap

A dispatch-created allocation already concludes through the supervisor's
terminal reconcile: it requests reservation release, and attempt conclusion
asks Worktrees to conclude the disposable dispatch-attempt workspace. Worktrees
preserves a workspace that still carries a live outbound claim, so a protected
task relationship that nobody closed after `COMPLETED` kept the workspace from
ever finalizing. The coordinator already exposed the exact reconcile route; no
production caller invoked it after completion.

## Change

The supervisor's terminal reconcile now calls the existing reconcile route for
the reservation's exact retained relationship event once its task is
`COMPLETED`, before requesting release (created allocations) or concluding the
attempt (other terminal reservations). A `closed` or `absent` answer proceeds;
any error or other state retains the reservation and claim, logs it, and
retries on the next cycle. Submitted tasks and reservations with no retained
event are unchanged. The supervisor never supplies a receipt; the original
ground owner still verifies retirement.

Review follow-up: the released-attempt path (bodies whose reservation was
already releasing) now settles before concluding, and a retired attempt whose
workspace cleanup was held for outstanding obligations is re-armed to pending
with a fresh budget once its task completes and the relationship closes, so a
confirmation that arrives after retirement still reaches finalization. Other
holds remain held.
The re-arm is a fenced compare-and-swap that clears a spent cleanup lease
(a cleanup held after a claimed retry keeps its expired token), and each cycle
bounds attempted revisits, failures included, with a rotating cursor.

## Validation

Source tests: actual queue and ground-owner settlement stays bound while
submitted and closes exactly once after independent confirmation, idempotently;
supervisor wiring settles before release, and writer failure or a still-bound
answer leaves the reservation unreleased. A held retired cleanup is not
re-armed while submitted, is re-armed exactly once after confirmation, and the
released-attempt path concludes only after settlement.

## Limits

Source-only. Installed/native qualification of permanent finalization, mixed
callers and production cohort admission remain open, and both parent efforts
remain Active.

## Follow-up: conclusion retry after priming

A clean-room review-to-completion lab on the merged settlement showed the
relationship closing as intended, but the workspace conclusion stayed pending.
The first attempt primed the record (status `complete`) and then deferred
removal because mux presence was unknown; every retry then failed before
reaching the idempotent conclusion, because the identity lookup rejected the
now-terminal row as "allocation owner unavailable". Dispatch-attempt
conclusion now admits a terminal row for its identity check only (reuse
still rejects terminal rows) and, once the same project and path are re-proven,
lets the existing idempotent `conclude-disposable` finish. An identity change,
an unavailable owner, or a missing or unreadable row still preserves the
workspace: project-scoped absence cannot prove the identity the owner's
all-project conclusion would act on. Regression tests cover the retry, the
retained identity check, preservation without an identity row and unchanged
reuse rejection.
