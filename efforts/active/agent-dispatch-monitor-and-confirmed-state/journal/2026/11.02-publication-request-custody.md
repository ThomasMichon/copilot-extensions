## Bounded source change

The existing supervisor sender retains each original client and concrete base
URL through reconnect. The ordinary client can reconnect independently, but
cannot silently retarget an unresolved metadata request or close its transport
under an in-flight future. Accepted recovery stays on the original target.
Later preparation also preserves the settled publisher's target rather than
rebasing identical-looking scope metadata from another coordinator.

The metadata and cohort preparation owners can explicitly discard only
confirmed-unaccepted originals: never-posted preparation or the actual
provider's definitive transaction refusal. Live futures, unacknowledged success,
generic statuses, unknown replies and earlier ambiguous POSTs remain accountable.
A cohort route can reject after committing its source recheck; its original
history must distinguish that accepted outcome from a rolled-back refusal.

Existing source/acquisition guards, discovery futures and shutdown retirement
own this work. There is no new timer, executor, lease, publisher registry or wire
version. A timed-out metadata future also retains its source-read and
declaration-publication children until their actual completion. No tick or
serve caller enables publication.

## Validation and remaining gate

Focused controls use the real HTTP client, ASGI provider, SQLite catalog,
singleton and future inventory to exercise lost replies, preflight/in-flight
reconnect, identical metadata at a replacement target, source/target changes,
unsupported peers, safe discard and retained unknown outcomes. Cohort controls
include both pre-write refusal and post-commit source-recheck rejection.
The neighboring supervisor reconnect, self-update and retirement flows remain
part of the source validation.
The bounded contained runs pass **357 distinct controls across ten selectors**;
all retained runs confirm owned cleanup. Five touched typed owners, touched
Python lint, documentation consistency and the installation contract pass.
These are source controls, not native rollout measurements.

The source receipt does not complete automatic configured-cohort publication,
per-scope reconciliation, ordinary allocator/initial-prompt migration,
suspended recovery, finalize-before-submission or installed/native qualification.
Cross-endpoint queue continuity still needs its owning integration before an
automatic publisher may follow a replacement endpoint. Both the parent Plan
and Validation Plan remain Active.
