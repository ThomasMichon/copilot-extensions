the existing rejection owner has rejected its exact consumed proposal. It does

another workspace. The [provider reference](../../../../../plugins/agent-dispatch/docs/task-obligations.md#internal-rejected-proposal-resubmission)

proposal. The retained lineage includes the prior preparation and rejection
references plus exact feedback; locked queue rechecks verify it before and
after a fresh independent readiness observation. Original assignment, task
claim, native and review policy remain unchanged.

[submission responsibility intent](../../../../../visions/plugins/agent-dispatch/task-outputs-and-review/README.md#submission-retains-worktree-responsibility)

There is no new externally advertised Dispatch syntax or capability, schema
ledger, execution owner, ordinary writer activation or cleanup permission.
Existing rollback-resistant completion and immutable-receipt guards stay in
place. Older preparers cannot replace the retained request.
