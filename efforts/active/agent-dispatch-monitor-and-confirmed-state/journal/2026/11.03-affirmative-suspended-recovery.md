## Source boundary

worker explicitly starts with its task generation, actual native and monotonic
recovery revision. The existing assignment owns counters and clocks; the
existing wake outbox and coordinator GC cadence own delivery and reconciliation.
The cold supervisor path no longer treats a process-resume result as acceptance.
Legacy non-pool continuation remains separate.

The selected three-attempt startup budget, five-minute nudges, thirty-minute
decision window and task-wide 1/3/6/12-hour cooldown are reused. Intentional
waits cancel old recovery requests without resetting debt. Holds freeze
policy clocks, including delivery observed before the next reconciliation.
Held steering remains durable without spending an attempt.

Unknown recovery sends retain the original leased-token custody on the same
wake, including outside a qualified producer cohort. They do not authorize a
replacement send or claim qualification. Exhaustion fences generation and
late affirmation, returns the task to `QUEUED` and retains its unresolved
reservation as `RELEASING`; it never stops a process or frees capacity.
Task-only grants admit the narrow current suspended `/pool-start` route, not
other suspended-worker operations. The client negotiates actual running
provider support before requesting revision-bound acceptance.
