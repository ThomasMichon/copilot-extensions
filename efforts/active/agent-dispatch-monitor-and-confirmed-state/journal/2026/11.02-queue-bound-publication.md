## Source boundary

The existing sender now connects reconnect to the queue's retained
`workspace_queue_identity`, not a new identity registry or speculative
prerequisite. Authenticated catalog reads preserve that identity; original
begin/publish requests retain it through recovery and the actual SQLite
transactions enforce it before mutation or idempotent result recovery.
Running-peer capability negotiation refuses an identity-pinned POST after
unsupported provider rollback; old request forms remain supported.

A quiescent original request can continue through another endpoint for that
same queue. Active flights and source children retain their original transports;
candidate reads and retired transports stay in the same existing future and
cleanup owner. Another reconnect during a candidate read cannot close that
transport or authorize a write. Same-looking foreign catalogs cannot rebase
pending or settled custody. Identity is checked before effectful declaration
collection as well as at fresh metadata POST admission.

This closes part of the Dispatch vision's pool-owned assignment/startup source
delta and independent coordinator/supervisor continuity. It follows existing-owner
supervision, bounded work and version-skew patterns without another timer,
executor, process, queue or lease. The default-off cadence remains off.
